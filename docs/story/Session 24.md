# Session 24: Eight Hours to Easthaven

The forge had gone quiet. The rest of Sunblight had not.

Somewhere beyond the walls, steel was meeting steel: the sound of a fortress deciding who it belonged to, now that [Xardorok](../world/characters/Xardorok%20Sunblight.md) lay dead beside his lever. [Grandolpha](../world/characters/Grandolpha%20Muzgardt.md) offered no comment on it. In the party's presence she did nothing at all. Nobody stayed to learn how it came out.

The elevator carried them up to the machinery room at the top, open to the sky. It was dusk. Far off, small against the last of the light, the dragon was flying toward [Dougan's Hole](../world/atlas/Dougan's%20Hole.md), following a channel someone had carved into a stone table.

[Aronious](../world/characters/Aronious.md) worked it out the way he read terrain. Dougan's Hole, then [Goodmead](../world/atlas/Goodmead.md), then [Easthaven](../world/atlas/Easthaven.md): eight hours for the dragon. For the party, in a straight line to Easthaven, eight hours.

That settled the question of rest.

It did not settle the question of the two small towns in between. Goodmead had no speaker. The last one had died in a verbeeg incursion, and the seat sat empty while a handful of hopefuls reached for it, [Hobbins](../world/characters/Hobbins.md)'s father among them. Dougan's Hole had [Edgra Dermoot](../world/characters/Edgra%20Dermoot.md), an old trapper woman. So it would be Edgra. Dougan's Hole would run for Easthaven and gather up Goodmead on the way.

---

Aronious scooped Hobbins up and ran for the dogsled. She cast _sending_ as she was carried. A dragon is coming. Eight hours. Get to Easthaven, and bring Goodmead's people with you.

The answer was curt. Dougan's Hole was leaving its houses where they stood.

Aronious drove and watched the sky. The dragon's flight was unsteady, and light seeped from between its plates where the Shield Guardian had hammered it, radiance leaking out of something that had been built to hold it in. The party slept in shifts on the moving sled while the dragon dropped behind them, bending away toward Dougan's Hole.

---

Eight hours later they came up on Easthaven's gate behind the last of the evacuees, a hundred and fifty or so from the two towns, carrying what they could. The damaged walls had been patched with packed ice and snow. Behind them stood two hundred militia: a hundred and fifty in uniform, fifty who had simply turned up.

[Imdra](../world/characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) left to join the commanders. [Yin Shen](../world/characters/Yin%20Shen.md) followed her. Hobbins went to the vanguard, waved forward by a commander who remembered her from the town hall after the first Duergar trouble, and Aronious made his report. The dragon was wounded. It was about thirty minutes out.

Smoke stood in the sky from the direction of Goodmead.

---

Yin Shen found an alley with no one in it.

She spoke to her sister, the one who had brought about her exile.

"I know I fucked up in the past. I don't ask much from you, but please, if you can, help these people out."

Snow began to fall. In a frozen puddle, the face looking back at her was not her own. It was golden and pale, there for a moment and then gone. Then the sigil of Tyr melted itself into the ice.

No weapon came. No spell. But she had been heard, and she walked out of the alley carrying something she had not carried in. She was not the only one. Elsewhere in Easthaven, the other two each caught a glimpse of a figure who looked a great deal like Yin Shen: one in a shiny rock at the front of the militia, one as a shape among the stars.

---

Imdra's writ and a deputy's standing got Aronious into a watchtower on the wall. From the top, the dragon was minutes away. Hobbins went back through Dzaan's notebook, looking for anything hidden. She found nothing.

Then the wall was gone. Radiant breath shattered it, and the dragon landed in front of the militia.

Yin Shen asked a commander for a healing potion.

"What do I look like, the Quartermaster? Head over to the barracks and maybe you'll find something there."

Imdra stayed bent over the maps.

Aronious got down from the tower before it crumbled. He healed Hobbins, then caught Yin Shen at the quartermaster's door and healed her scorched arm. The quartermaster, frightened, handed over the last three healing potions in Easthaven's stores. Two went with Yin Shen, one with Aronious. One of hers would be empty before the night was over.

---

The dragon's breath took a section of the vanguard and left nothing where it had been. The militia answered with spears, stones, and arrows, and most of it bounced off.

Hobbins threw the strongest chromatic orb she had. Even with whatever had come down with the snow behind it, it went wide. She fell back behind the troops.

A militiaman got his spear under one of the plates and pried. It opened. Something bright leaked out.

Hobbins's next orb was lightning, and it did not miss. It blew a gaping wound in the dragon's flank. The Shield Guardian went to work on that flank with both fists, then grabbed the tail and was dragged about for its trouble. Aronious spent the last of his magic on a blessing for Yin Shen, and she took to the rooftops.

---

Aronious lashed the dragon's helm-like jaw shut with _thorn whip_, and militia took hold of the vines and hauled. The next breath had nowhere to go. It went off inside, and the chardalyn around the dragon's head cracked.

It nearly tore free. He held it with everything he had, including the part that had not been his an hour ago.

The dragon made them pay for it. Tail, claws, and a buffet from an ice-laden wing battered the Shield Guardian, Aronious, his reindeer, and the militia. The line thinned to eighty or ninety.

Hobbins's second lightning orb widened the wound, and behind it sat the core: a small black hole, or a dead star.

"Everyone try to hit that weak spot I just exposed."

---

Yin Shen leapt from a rooftop onto the dragon's wing, ran up its back, and drove her short sword into a vent in its neck, pinning the head. The Shield Guardian and the reindeer kept striking.

Aronious handed the vines to the militia. He drew the giant's bone longbow further than he had ever drawn it and sent an arrow clean through the core.

The body began to swell. Yin Shen sprinted off its back toward a building. Hobbins spent the last magic she had on a _thunderwave_ that drove the blast back into the dragon and away from the militia standing nearest.

The dragon shattered into flecks too small to hold its magic. It left a crater and a missing section of wall. Around eighty militia were still standing. So was the reindeer.

---

Hobbins was first into the crater. There was nothing in it but chardalyn fragments.

Aronious found Imdra and told her what the material does to a mind with prolonged contact, a kind of necrosis, and that it should be buried rather than handled. A task force would see to the cleanup. Until then, militia would keep the townsfolk away from the site.

The party found an inn. Yin Shen offered a prayer of thanks to her sister.

Then they slept, all of them at once.
