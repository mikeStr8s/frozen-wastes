# Session 14: The Return to Easthaven

The party left the Duergar stronghold with the Duergar leader bound to the dogsled, stolen goods from [Caer-Konig](../world/atlas/Caer-Konig.md) piled on top of him. The two rescued goats were tied to the sled, making the journey back slow and cumbersome.

As they passed the ravine where they had seen the ancient white dragon slumbering, they noticed the wyrm was no longer visible — its massive form appeared to have been re-buried by fresh snowdrifts.

---

They returned to Caer-Konig, and [Aronious](../world/characters/Aronious.md) and [Yin Shen](../world/characters/Yin%20Shen.md) took the prisoner and the magical color-changing lantern to [The Northern Light](../world/atlas/The%20Northern%20Light.md) to meet with the town Speaker and return the stolen goods.

[Hobbins](../world/characters/Hobbins.md) was left with the dogsled at the north end of town. With the sled caretakers gone, the dogs became restless and began to wander. Hobbins' small body had little effect on the reins, and the dogs moved of their own accord down the streets of Caer-Konig. With squeaks and yelps, she wrestled with the leather cords and pulled with all her might to guide them back to their kennel. The kennel master saw this unfold and walked out to greet her. Hobbins asked him to watch their things, tossed him a copper coin, and ran off to catch up with the party.

---

[Allie and Cori](../world/characters/Cori%20and%20Allie.md) were thrilled to see their lantern again but apologized for their lack of coin. They rummaged through their belongings and handed over a small purse containing only three gold and six silver pieces.

"We're sorry we don't have more to offer you," Cori said sadly. "Times is rough with the winter's Rime shuttering travel, trade, and the joy in our hearts. This is all we have left."

"We can offer you rooms and food free of charge whenever you visit!" Allie quickly added.

The conversation then turned to the prisoner.

Yin Shen explained the situation at the frozen stronghold. [Trovus](../world/characters/Trovus.md) was shocked to hear of such a grand conspiracy. "And here I thought I was looking for petty thieves," he muttered, taking a long pull from his wine bottle.

Yin Shen asked if he would be willing to speak to other town Speakers and help them understand the threat. "I can do you one better!" Trovus exclaimed. "Cori! Quill and parchment, if you don't mind."

Despite the drink, his hand was steady and his words clear. In short order, he drafted a legal document **deputizing the party as agents of Caer-Konig**, placing them as direct reports to the town Speaker. "This should go a long way in proving you've got our backing," he said, rolling his signet ring in the wax seal.

Yin Shen stored the writ safely in her bag, then asked if he could help with the cost of travel in the area — she suspected they had a lot of journeying ahead.

Trovus dug through his heavy furs and fine Speaker's clothing, emptying pockets onto the counter. He pulled out four separate half-empty containers of alcohol before producing what he was looking for. "Ahh, here we are." He handed Yin Shen another slip — a **Torg's Trading Company Travel Voucher**. Fine print indicated it was valid for Eastway travel but did not service Lonelywood or Dugan's Hole.

"That should go a ways for you," he said. "It's no silver bullet, but it'll get you across Ten-Towns no problem."

---

The party left the inn and returned to where Hobbins had left the sled. When they arrived, the sled was packed away, the dogs were kenneled and resting, and their goods sat on a pallet waiting for them. The porter was dozing in a chair nearby, hat resting over his eyes.

Aronious took immediate issue with this slothful behavior. He scolded the porter about the risk of thieves while he slept, then accused him of being a thief himself — that this was all part of a plan to cheat them. The porter, confused, apologized profusely for his lack of professionalism. "You're right, sir! I should never have gone lax once my work was done!"

Aronious continued berating the man until Hobbins stepped in and said they needed to leave.

"You're right, Hobbins, we do need to leave," Aronious declared. "But not before I leave a terrible review on **Gelp**!"

---

The party made their way to the caravan office and, after some trouble proving ownership of the voucher, secured free passage to [Easthaven](../world/atlas/Easthaven.md).

Yin Shen stayed alert the entire journey to ensure their prisoner did not escape.

Once in Easthaven, they went straight to the town hall. There, they met [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../world/characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) and presented her not only with a new prisoner but also with the signed and sealed document from Trovus.

Yin Shen explained the conspiracy in full. Everything aligned with the thieves who had previously escaped from Easthaven. She unfolded the letter they had found, which stated the Duergar were using the **Easthaven Ferry** as a base of operations.

Imdra saw the looming threat clearly and believed them. She promised to send notices to all town Speakers and to do what she could to prepare — though she warned it would take time, as not everyone moved as quickly as she did.

Yin Shen asked if they could be deputized in the same way they had been in Caer-Konig. Imdra held out her hand for Trovus' document, read it, then wrote that while not formally deputized, the party was **working under official business of Captain Imdra and Easthaven**. She signed it and pressed her signet into wax.

---

The party decided it was time to visit the music shop owner. They first went to **Drums Emporium**. The door was closed and locked. No lights shone from inside. The shelves were empty, and dust motes floated in the air. The back room door hung askew, its interior shadowed in darkness.

Aronious kicked down the door in fear and haste. He barged in, shocked by the emptiness, then rushed to the back workshop area. It too was clean — no wood shavings, no metal dust. The broken trapdoor had been replaced with seamless, newly installed flooring.

The party panicked and ran full speed to **Strings and Sundries**. Lights glowed inside, and instruments lined the shelves.

Aronious attempted to kick this door down as well, but his boot bounced off and he hurt his ankle. He readied his other leg when Yin Shen tried the doorknob. The door swung open just as Aronious kicked — and he fell forward into the shop.

The shopkeeper was a tall, slender woman in a red dress. She was startled to see them tumble inside. "Oh! Umm, may I help you?"

The party frantically skipped formalities, wanting to know where the gnome was. She giggled and explained she was the new store manager — the gnome owner had decided to step back from day-to-day operations. "He's probably at home, behind the workshop. You can find him there."

---

The party sprinted to his home. Aronious charged forward, leg readied to kick down the door. Yin Shen knelt and pulled out lockpicking tools, working on the lock. Hobbins scrambled with her little paws, trying to squeeze through gaps in the doorframe.

After much commotion and little success, the door finally opened from the inside.

[Polard Hinscrop](../world/characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md) stood in the doorway, hands on his hips, looking annoyed. "What are you guys doing here?"

They explained they had gone on a short trip north and had returned to see how he was doing.

"I waited for you to come back and take me on an adventure," he said accusingly, "and now I find you went on one without me!"

The party apologized profusely, saying they'd gotten things mixed up — they thought he needed more time to think it over.

He forgave them, their apologies seeming sincere. The party immediately invited him to join them.

Polard scurried into his house. Sitting on a chair at the dining room table was a fully handmade suit of armor: the helmet was a metal pasta strainer, the shoulder pads were bent cymbals, and the rest was a hodgepodge of kitchen utensils, musical instrument components, and leather straps.

He put everything on, then disappeared and reappeared with a backpack twice his size. "Ready to go!"

---

The party decided to visit the smith. They had heard the dwarves there were able to enchant materials.

Once there, they learned the dwarves didn't do the enchanting themselves — they had connections and shipped goods off for arcane modification before re-importing them to sell. They acted as middlemen, and it was good for business to keep those connections secret.

The party was determined to get Polard better armor, convinced his homemade gear wouldn't be sufficient. The dwarves inspected it and were genuinely impressed by the craftsmanship. Sure, it looked silly — but they promised it was well-made and would stop a blade.

After some bartering, Hobbins offered to teach one of the dwarven apprentices — the one most adept at magic — how to use the arcane to reinforce the edge of a mundane metal sword. In exchange, the dwarves would provide one steel helmet free of charge.

Yin Shen took the opportunity to purchase some caltrops as well.

---

Aronious decided to investigate the local fletcher. He entered and saw a solitary elf — tall but lacking the usual elegance associated with his kind. He was heavyset with a bushy gray beard, lazily attaching feathers to an arrow shaft and barely lifting his eyes when Aronious entered.

Most of the bows were regular or mundane, but there was a section with intricately carved scrimshaw on bone reinforcing the limbs. The price was steep — far beyond what Aronious could afford.

After a brief discussion with the shopkeeper, Hobbins walked in and browsed alongside Aronious. She saw the bone reinforcement and asked about it. He explained it made the bow stronger, and if they could provide materials, the fletcher would only charge for labor.

Hobbins reached into her bag and pulled out the **frost giant bone** she had been holding onto. She placed it on the shopkeeper's table. "Will this work?" she asked sweetly.

The elf's eyes went wide. "By the gods, is this... giant's bone?" He held his hands over it and used arcane magic to identify the material. "Frost giant's bone. Incredible."

His face, previously stoic and uninterested, was now alight with joy and determination. "Get out of my shop at once! Return in one week — I will have a bow the likes of which you have never seen."

The party saw the flames in his eyes and did not argue. The moment they stepped outside, they heard frantic movement, the click of locks, and the slamming of wooden shutters.

The fletcher's shop was now closed.
