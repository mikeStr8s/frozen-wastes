# Session 26: Squidlings in the Snow

The poison took a few minutes to wear off.

[Yin Shen](../world/characters/Yin%20Shen.md) spent them in the snow where she had fallen. [Aronious](../world/characters/Aronious.md) and [Hobbins](../world/characters/Hobbins.md) left her to it.

When she could move again, she sat for an hour and cast _find familiar_. What came of it was an owl.

She sent it over the valley. The ship was three levels tall and half buried, with two ballistas and a long scar in the snow behind it where it had come down. Its shell was semi-translucent. There was light behind it, and things moving. The burrowers' tunnels ran fifteen or twenty feet from where they had taken her and then stopped. Of the creatures themselves, the owl found nothing.

So they went down to the ship.

---

Yin Shen went up by grappling hook to the lower outer deck. The double doors were chitin and iron, and frozen shut.

Hobbins took out the sea hag's frozen eye and used it to cast _detect evil and good_. Five aberrations. Three in the room behind the door, one on the second level, one at the very top.

The Shield Guardian vaulted onto the deck. Yin Shen melted the ice with kitsune fire, and under it was a simple mechanical latch. She looked for a trap and found none.

She eased the door open.

---

Through the gap she saw crates, and among them three small things with tentacles for faces, smaller than [Polard](../world/characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md), playing the way children play. A flesh golem was hauling boxes around them. It had been stitched together from goblins, dwarves, and reindeer.

The door opened the rest of the way. The golem charged. The squidlings retreated to the back of the room.

Yin Shen stepped inside with the psi crystal held out in front of her and offered peace. The squidlings fixed on the crystal. One of them scurried up the stairs.

The golem was less interested. Aronious and his deer companion wounded it, and so did Hobbins's _magic missile_. Then it hit Yin Shen, and the blow had lightning in it.

Then the wall came in. The Shield Guardian came through it and let go of the divine smite Aronious had stored there. The golem's midsection was destroyed. It went down and stayed down, though it was not dead.

They left it that way.

---

Hobbins cast _comprehend languages_ and asked the two that remained what had happened.

They answered the way toddlers do.

"Ship crash. Stuck. Need help."

Hobbins offered to help. The squidlings calmed, and pointed a tentacle toward the stairs, where the third had gone.

---

On the middle deck, Yin Shen sent the owl on up to the helm. It found somewhere to hide, and nobody noticed it. Under a lattice of glowing jellyfish, a ceremorph the size of a gnome was working at a control panel.

Hobbins, meanwhile, had heard Gnomish through a door. She knocked, and went in alone.

[Voren](../world/characters/Voren.md) was a ceremorph too, gnome-sized, and he was repairing the ship. It had been a research mission, he told her. They collected creatures: two carrion crawlers, and a larger beast that tunnels. After the crash there had been no way to feed them, so they had let all three go.

They answered, he said, to something called the mother brain. He insisted they were not controlled, not the way other mind flayers are.

Then his scanner found a power source. It was on the other side of the wall, which was where Yin Shen was standing.

"The visitors on the ship, yes, they might be able to help. I sure hope our golem hasn't done any damage to them."

---

Hobbins asked whether he would come to the party's aid if she called by _sending_.

"No, I don't think we can do that. We gotta get out of here. We've already caused too much trouble being stuck in this area. If someone discovers us, they'll definitely take us down."

Yin Shen came in with the crystal. Voren stopped using his mouth and spoke inside their heads instead. He named it a psi crystal, and showed them the one in the ship's propulsion, burnt out. She told him hers had come from the skull of a mind flayer, in a mine.

The party asked about payment. Future transport was refused. An item was not.

At the helm, [Dredavex](../world/characters/Dredavex.md), the one the owl had been watching, brought out a small chitinous chest. Inside were a pair of crystal lenses and a helmet shaped like an owl, capped with something very like a brain.

Hobbins tried the lenses. They were too big for her and far from comfortable, but she could see in the dark, and she could pick out more of what she was looking at. Then she tried the helmet. It wanted attunement. She took both off again.

The ceremorphs were not upset about their golem. They would rather get off the planet than dwell on it.

Yin Shen handed over the psi crystal. The lenses and the owl came back the other way.
