- the party returned to Bremen with Spellix, he thanked them for saving him from the goblins and informed them he'd be staying at the inn for a short time to recover before heading south to return to his family. he pulled out his pockets to reveal they were empty and shrugged saying that maybe the inn was too much to hope for.
- Aronious gives him 2 gold so that rest can come easy
- The party sends Dzaan off to the inn as well to rest and recover, he enters and starts manically scribing in his book again.
- Yin Shen remarks that its about time they all rest up, everyone took a beating. Hobbins and Aronious scowl at Yin Shen as she escaped unscathed
- Hobbins notices the Shield guardian is still covered in Goblin grafiti and wants to find a store to buy paints to clean him up a bit.
- They browse the shelves of the local market and find "Grizwalds Famous Degreaser" with the tagline "It'll remove anything and everything". The clerk informs them its actually terrible at removing grease but cuts through paint as if it was never there. The party decides against it since its too harsh.
- Instead they decide to just buy paint to paint over the gobin writing. Green, Pink, Purple, and Black are chosen to embolden and highlight the constructs curves and edges. While also attempting to tone down the menicing nature of such a large metal construct.
- Yin Shen suggests they all head in for rest since they all could use the recovery. Aronious hardly tries to hold Hobbins back from biting at Yin Shen's ankles. All the while squeaking "not a single arrow hit you", "we're covered in bruises", "shield guardian almost destroyed".
- Once in the Inn the party starts making their way to their rooms when the door bursts open and Mylbor walks in with a crimson stained sack draped over one shoulder.
- Yin Shen greets him and he recounts his adventure hunting and taking down the red yeti. "Only took one shot, though I only had one shot" he finishes with a hearty laugh holding up his Thunder Stick. The end now mushroomed out with singed and curling pieces of metal.
- Mylbor invites them all to have a drink to celebrate his successful hunt and tosses down his coin purse. He passes a tankard to Yin Shen and Aronious, Aronious choosing to pour a bottle cap full of clear liquor for Hobbins.
- After a hearty "cheers!" Mylbor downs his drink in a single go, egging on the rest of the party to follow suit.
- Aronious thinking additional alcohol might make Mylbor more pliable decides to challenge him to a drinking contest. He agrees eagerly.
- Aronious grabs a whole bottle of clear liquor and downs it in a single go. Mylbor asks the barmaid how much ale that equates and she says a whole cask. Mylbor beams and runs behind the counter and lays on the ground. Yin Shen grabs the handle of the keg and lets loose a torrent of Ale.
- Mylbor sputters and drinks but eventually taps out. He slowly stands and thumps his chest letting out a belch. "You win laddy!"
- Yin Shen suggests its time to sleep since Mylbor will be feeling the affects of the alcohol soon and the party is still needing to recover from their adventure. Hobbins no longer able to contain herself lunges at Yin Shen and Aronious does nothing to stop her as they scuffle.
- The party heads to bed soon after things settle down and arrise early in the morning.
- They wait for Mylbor to wake up and when he does Yin Shen and him come to an agreement to contact each other should either of them hear of information regarding their goals. Yin Shen wanting information on the whereabouts of the ancient white dragon that lives in the Dale and Mylbor wanting information on trophy worthy hunts or advisaries.
- Parting ways with Mylbor the group heads to Termalaine, a town they've not been to yet.
- Though traveling takes most of the day the party arrives in the evening to find a large Orc man in fine clothing barking orders at a small town militia. Most if not all newly conscripted.
- The party approach him and explain how they are Imdra's deputies and are looking for the town speaker. He introduces himself as the town speaker Oarus Masthew. He has been training his local townsfolk to defend themselves and the town ever since Imdra sent word of a looming threat and the Druergar incursion.
- Happy to see the town working to defend themselves the party leaves Masthew to continue to train the guards.
- Finding a place to think about next steps they all agree that Hobbins attempting to contact the Ancient White Dragon is a good idea. Yin Shen is confident that white dragons are benevolent and enjoy the company of humans.
- Hobbins uses Sending to contact the dragon. "dont panic, we are friendly, need help, ten towns in danger meet at redwaters, lupine can vouch, at sundown in 2 days. ciao!"
- In response in a deep rasping growl "Come to my lair at the Sea of Moving Ice. You'll find a shipwreck I use as one of my homes."
- With only this vague direction the party knows that they will need about two day's travel before reaching the sea.
- They mull over options while walking through town.
- The party sees a Gelp board with a posting "Miners Lost! 50g Reward", then in smaller text "Local miners guild refusing work until lost miners are found and mines made safe"
- Low on funds they consider but ultimately resign themselves to heading out into the cold wilderness once again.
