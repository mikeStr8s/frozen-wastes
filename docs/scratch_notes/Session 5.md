## 💀 6. Sea Hag’s Frozen Eye

**Wondrous item, uncommon (cursed)**

A milky eyeball encased in clear ice. It floats in water like a buoy.

- Once per day, you can use the eye to cast **Detect Magic** or **Detect Evil and Good** (your choice).
    
- While attuned, you gain **darkvision 30 ft underwater**.
    
- **Curse:** When you use the eye, you must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or see brief visions of drowning — granting disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks until your next long rest.


## 🐚 1. Whispering Conch

**Wondrous item, common**  
A chipped, frost-crusted seashell that hums softly when held to the ear.

- When you listen to the conch, you can faintly hear distant ocean waves and muffled screams beneath them.
    
- Once per day, if you whisper a question into the shell while near open water, the sea will answer with a single _emotion_ — warmth (yes), cold (no), or silence (uncertain).
    

**Flavor:** It’s unclear whether the hag made the shell, or found it. It sometimes hums on its own, as though remembering the tides.

- The party, flush with victory from the giants skeleton they take time to celebrate before deciding to continue across the room to the exit on the other side.
- the room exits into a tunnel that splits in opposite directions, party hears humming coming from the right tunnel and choose to travel left.
- The party ends up back at the same frozen river but much further down its flows. Before stepping out onto the ice the party looks around and investigates the frosty corridor.
- Hobbins notices the four large giants skeletons frozen in the solid ice beneath them. Their eyes slowing kindling with blue flame. Much to her joy, the ice was completely solid and crystalline. The skeletons could not break free.
- The party walks out on the ice and hear the humming again coming from the right. They decide to investigate.
- Party walks onto a gravely beach where they see a pallid and greyish elderly woman. As she hums the sound of her dull, rusty cleaver thumping against the cuttingboard begins to rise. She stands near a pile of gore and is chopping organs, meat, and bone.
- Next to her is a large cauldron bubbling away with her vile concoction.
- "Ahh, visitors", she turned and sneered
- "Let granny Maud prepare you some dinner", she then advanced on the party
- Aronious readied his bow and took fire, Hobbins threw spells and Yin Shen attacked from the shadows.
- Their collective assault on Maud was tremendous.
- Her facade broke and the elderly woman shed from her like snake skin. A large, ugly, blue sea hag emerged and the shock of this caused Hobbins fortitude to faulter. Too afraid to approach any further.
- The swift actions of Aronious and Yin Shen broke the spell and in the end victory was had for the party.
- As Maud fell dying to the ground, a small blue orb fell from her hand.
- Each party member swiped for the pearl, but Hobbins emerged successful. No mere trinket, She held the sea hags frozen eye.
