- Yin Shen was formerly a demigod, daughter to a minor diety of order and law who devoted herself to serving Tyr
- Yin Shen was a god of tricks, pranks, joyous laughter. Some children who knew of her existance would whisper her name while hiding and waiting for their prank to succeed.
- her fall came when one prank too many was pulled on the pantheon itself. She was pranking her sister, another demigod, who also served Tyr.
- Yin Shen gave her blessing to a festival patron, her divine station did not allow her the foresight to see what that would result in since she is a demigod.
- She had hoped that it would liven up the events of the evening and be a bit of fun, but the festival ended in flames.
- Offerings and prayers no longer going to the heavens and some mortals injured, Yin Shen's sister was quick to report this to Tyr
- The next thing Yin Shen knew, she was hurtling toward the earth.
