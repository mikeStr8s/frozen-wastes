- The party brings the kobolds to the foreman and he is hesitent. He needs to check with the town speaker first. He returns with the speaker and the speaker formally accepts the kobolds into the town. They will get food and shelter in exchange for work in the mines.
- the speaker oarus pulls the party to the town hall after to discuss. He presents grave news of an attack on Easthaven where Imdra was taken. Termalaine experienced invaders as well and they captured a duergar and were able to get information on where the stronghold was. Deep in the mountains of The Spine Of The World
- Yin Shen wants to go to an armorer to see if the grell tentacles are of any use. Karthax the dragonborn armorer thinks that he may be able to add leather bindings to make a suitable whip. The party is told to return in the morning for the finished weapon.
- Yin Shen also wants to find a jewler to appraise her gemstone she pried from the rocks in the mine. She finds the mines own appraiser and enters his shop. He is a wiry, balding man with a nasaly voice. He was astonished at the quality and determined the value to be nearing 100g. He was keen to buy it from her but Yin Shen turned him down and left soon after.
- The party rests at the local inn
- In the morning Yin Shen picks up the whip from Karthax
- Hobbins uses Sending to contact Mylbor and recruit him to take down the duergar, he responds eagerly and heads right to town meeting up with them later in the evening kicking in the door to the inn and loudly announcing himself.
- Now together they all travel to the fortress by way of the Eastway. Passing Easthaven as they travel they see the remnants of battle damage being reparied. The large front gate blackened by fire and splintered by great force or magic. They redoubled their speed to the fortess.
- With Aronious guiding the party through the snow and tundra they arrived in the foothills and base of the mountain. Again he was able to steer them well and avoid avalanche and freezing cold.
- They travel deep and high into the mountains until finally sheer fortress walls rose above them. carved directly from the stone of the mountain it was riddled with glowing arrowslits. A narrow slippery stair lead up along the side to a large stone door.
- They arrive and as they approach the door they hear a sound from within as though a lock clicking open.
- Once stepping in a crossbow bolt shoots from an arrowslit at the party, ambushing them at the entrance. Nine Duergar stationed in the barracks at the front gates begin to rally and grow large. Hobbins hurls chromatic orbs, their magics ricocheting from duergar to duergar. Aronious sent arrows flying back through the arrowslit while sending his shield guardian to block the doorway. Yin Shen tossed caltrops into the doorway so all those who tried to exit would be hindered. The hall and room was narrow. Too close for the whole group to navigate Mylbor and Polard were of little use in these tight quarters.
- once the battle resolve they continued onward. They found another chamber just off the entrance where a table with a map was arrayed. In a room beyond they found a large ornate bed and a large chest at the foot. Yin Shen was quick to pick the lock and eager to extract the gem encrusted garments within. Once all items were recovered a hatch in the bottom sprung and poison gas flooded the room.
- Hobbins quickly closed the door to contain the gas as Yin Shen hammered on the other side. Aronious attempted to pull hobbins from the door to relieve Yin Shen but Hobbins shook him off violently. Only after pleading from Yin Shen, Aronious, and Polard did Hobbins relent and release the hold on the door. Yin Shen emerged gasping. She collapsed and shuddered on the ground, near death she took some time to recover.
- The party investigated the map room more discovering a small closet hidden away. Inside were multiple mind flayer skulls and at the center a chardalyn statuete. Hobbins sends a crossbow bolt at the statue, shattering it.
- Yin Shen touches a skull and freezes in place. To her time is passing by in days, weeks, years, eons. Trapped inside her mind getting pulled ever northward while never moving. To the rest of the party she is stuck in place, hand outstreched, finger hardly touching the skull. Aronious and Hobbins shake her loose and once no longer touching she returns unharmed.
- They turn their investigation back to the map. It is an intricate carved table representing all of Ten Towns and the surrounding wilderness. A channel carved in the table holds a dragon figurine suspended on an iron pole. The channel linking all ten towns and indicating the flight path of the dragon. Turning a crank on the side moves the dragon around the table.
