- The party left Caer Konig stocked with winter adventuring gear following the large footprints and sled tracks.
- While traveling the wind and snow picked up quite a bit reducing visibility heavily. The rime is fighting back
- The party travels for hours through the rolling snow covered hills before passing over a larger hill. They see a ravine not too far in the distance and in the clearing in the center lies the back half of a polar bear. Seemingly by a single bite.
- Deciding to take a second and scan the area for threats the party hears a low rumble. The snow drift on the far wall of the ravine shifts and falls away in a small avalanche revealing the massive head and neck of an ancient white dragon.
- Aronious takes a deeper look and sees a saddle peeking out of the snow, strapped in place is a robed figure half buried still in the snow. The figure does not move, the head is slumped and the arms are down at its sides.
- Hobbins had heard of an ancient white dragon during her time in Ten Towns but had never had reason to believe the tales till now.
- The three decide to continue on their way quietly as to not disturb the sleeping wyrm.
- After more travel to Kelvin's Cairn the party arrives at the base of the mountain. They quickly hide the dogsled and their presence in the area and notice that there is a stronghold carved in the stone.
- First finding a guard outpost, there is no external entrance save for the barred windows. But inside a Duergar stands half dozing, unaware of the party.
- Yin Shen squeezes her way into the bars and falls to the floor. The Duergar is alerted and begins to rush to a trapdoor in the center of the room. Aronious shoots an arrow and with druidic magics calls roots from the earth to restrain who the arrow hits. The Duergar is wrapped up and Yin Shen goes for a non-lethal strike to knock them unconscious.
- After looting the Duergar the party heads down the trap door and it takes them down a tunnel leading into the mountain itself. After a short walk they ascend another ladder and find themselves just inside the mountain with arrowslits facing the guard building they were just in.
- Inside a guard is patrolling. Yin Shen hides in the shadows using minor illusion to disguise herself as a crate. Aronious seeing that this does not fool the patrolling guard thinks quick and sends another enchanted arrow flying, roots again restraining the duergar in place. Hobbins rushes from behind the corner and with a squeak blasts them with a frozen chromatic orb. The impact of it freezing their core in its entirety and blasting a frozen hole straight through them.
- The party continued to explore the stronghold. They found the main entrance and at the far end of that room was a larger than normal dogsled and a zombie ogre in a large iron cage. In an adjacent cage was the missing goats that were stolen from Caer Konig
- Continuing to explore, the party found a hallway with a lever at the end. The party investigated and pulling the lever caused spikes to shoot from the floor and ceiling, blocking the hallway behind them.
- After pulling the lever they hear a commotion in the room next to them. They hide the spikes and disperse themselves to avoid being seen. Setting a trap so that they can lure whoever is in the room to the area with the spikes.
- A duergar dressed in finery instead of armor walks from the room to investigate the noise. Walking to the end of the hallway. Hobbins pulls the lever and manages to pin his leg and prevent him moving. Yin Shen quickly dispatches attacks with her dagger, while not lethal bring him to an inch of his life.
- Hobbins and Yin Shen attempt interrogation. They get back vulgarities and arguments. Finally in dwarvish he yells "My father is more powerful than any of you filthy creatures could imagine. You don't understand the glory of it, the power found in the ice. None of you do. But you will learn soon enough when my father unleashes his terror upon Ten-Towns! Your doom soars on dragon's wings!"
- The party investigates the room the duergar was in and finds the magic lantern that was missing. Yin Shen also finds a crumpled letter. "Brother, You will find me on the frozen ferry in Easthaven. From this new base, the search for chardalyn continues. Long may our father reign over this dark land! -- Durth"
- The party resigns to load up all the stolen goods and bring those to Caer Konig. They also load up the final Duergar to bring to Easthaven.
- Before leaving Aronious decides to explore a final hallway. He discovers a holding cell area where there are denizens of ten towns who have been transformed into fungal monsters. Deciding to leave them alone Aronious continues on. There he finds the sleeping quarters of two more Duergar. They rest in their bed. Hobbins and Aronious quickly and quietly take them out to eliminate the threat they pose to Ten Towns.
- The party regroups and begins their trek back.
