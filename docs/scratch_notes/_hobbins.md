Hobbins' parents are minor nobles who are attempting to get a foothold in the politics of Waterdeep. They are not well known or well connected. Your family traveled from far away with nothing but an astonishingly large chest of gold and a dream. You were raised in Waterdeep with by your parents. You had an afinity for magic when young, got training with a wizard before getting the bright idea of selling magical items in Ten Towns. You never finished your schooling, that is why you were a level 1 wizard in Ten Towns when the adventure started.
