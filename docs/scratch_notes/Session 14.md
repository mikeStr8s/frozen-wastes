- The party leaves the Duergar stronghold with the Duergar leader bound to the dog sled. The stolen goods from Caer Konig piled on top of him.
- The travel back was slow with the two goats tied to the sled.
- The party wanted to pass by the ravine where they saw the ancient white dragon slumbering. They don't see the dragon, but its form looks to have been re-buried by snow drifts.
- They return to Caer Konig and Aronious and Yin Shen take the prisoner and magical color changing lantern to The Northern Light to hopefully meet with the town speaker and to return the stolen goods.
- Hobbins is left with the dog sled at the north of the town. With the sled caretakers gone, the dogs become restless and start to wander. Hobbins small body has little effect on the reigns and the dogs are beginning to move of their own accord down the streets of Caer Konig. With squeaks and yelps she is able to wrestle with the leather cords and with all her might pull to guide them back to their kennel. The kennel master sees this unfold and walks out to greet her. Hobbins asks that he watch their things, tosses him a copper coin, and runs off to catch up with the party.
- Allie and Cori are thrilled to see their lantern again and apologize for their lack of coin. They rummage for all they can find and hand over a coin purse with only 3 gold and 6 silver coins. "We're sorry we don't have more to offer you. Times is rough with the winters rime shuttering travel, trade, and the joy in our hearts. This is all we have left." says Cori.
  "We can offer you rooms and food free of charge whenever you visit!" Allie quickly interjects.
- The conversation moves to the prisoner.
- Yin Shen explains the situation at the frozen stronghold. Torvus is shocked to hear of such a grand conspiracy. "And here I thought I was looking for petty theives" he says after taking a long drunken pull from his wine bottle.
- Yin Shen asks if he's willing to talk to other town speakers and get them to understand. "I can do you one better! Cori! Quill and parchment if you don't mind". Drink had not slowed his hand or dulled his use of words. In a short time a legal document deputizing the party as agents of Caer Konig is drafted. It has them as direct reports to the town speaker. "This should go a long way in proving you've got our backing." he says while rolling his signet ring in the wax blob.
- Yin Shen takes the writ and stores it safely in her bag. She then asks if he can help with the cost of travel in the area. She thinks that they've got a lot of travel going on in their future.
- He digs through his heavy furs and his finer town speaker clothing. Emptying pockets onto the counter. He pulls four separate half empty containers of alcohol out before producing what he was looking for. "Ahh, here we are." as he hands another slip to Yin Shen it reads "Torg's Trading Company Travel Voucher". There is some additional fine print about how this is for Eastway travel and does not service Lonelywood or Dugan's Hole.
- "That should go a ways for you, its no silver bullet but it'll get you across Ten Towns no problem"
- The party leaves the inn and heads to where Hobbins left the sled. When they arrive the sled is packed away, the dogs are kenneled and resting, and their goods were sitting on a pallet waiting for them. The porter waiting for their return was dozing in a chair nearby with a hat resting over his eyes.
- Aronious took immediate issue with this slothful nature. He scolded the porter about the risk of thieves while he slept. Then accused him of being a thief and this was all part of a plan to cheat them. The porter was confused but apologized profusely for his lack of professionalism. "You're right sir! I should have never gone lax once my work had been done!". Aronious berated the man until Hobbins stepped in and said they needed to leave. "You're right Hobbins, we do need to leave. Not before I leave a terrible review on Gelp!"
- The party went off to the caravan office and although they had some trouble with proof of ownership with the voucher, they were able to get free passage to Easthaven.
- Yin Shen was sure to stay alert the entire way so that their prisoner did not escape.
- Once in Easthaven they went straight to the town hall. There they met Captain Imdra and were able to not only supply her with a new prisoner, but they have a document from Torvus that is signed and sealed with his name and signet.
- Yin Shen explains the conspiracy and everything lines up with the theives in Easthaven that previously escaped. She unfolds the letter she found that says they are using the Easthaven Ferry as a base.
- Imdra sees the threat looming and believes the party. She says she will be sending notices to all town speakers and will be doing what she can to prepare. This will take time and not everyone moves as quickly as she can.
- Yin Shen asks if they would be able to be deputized in the same way as they were in Caer Konig. Imdra holds out her hand asking for the document from Torvus. Imdra writes down that while not deputized, they are working under official business of Captain Imdra and Easthaven. She then puts her signature and signet in wax.
- The party decides that this is a good time to visit the music shop owner. They first go to Drums Imporioum. The door is closed and locked. There are no lights from inside. The Shelves are empty and dust motes float in the air. The back room door is askew but the interior of the room is shadowed in darkness.
- Aronious kicks down the door in fear and haste. He barges in and is shocked at the emptiness. He rushes to the back workshop area where it again is clean. No wood shavings, no metal dust. The broken trapdoor is a seamless wooden floor newly replaced.
- The party panics and runs full speed to Strings and Sundries. There are lights within and instruments on the shelves.
- Aronious attempts to kick this door down, but his boot bounces off and he hurts his ankle. He readys his other leg when Yin Shen Tries the doorknob. The door swings open just as Aronious is going to kick and he falls forward into the shop.
- The shopkeeper is a tall slender woman in a red dress and she is surprised to have them fall into the shop like that. "Oh! Umm, may I help you?"
- The party frantically skips formalities or greetings wanting to know where the gnome is. She giggles and says that shes the new store runner/manager and that the gnome owner decided to step back from the day to day. "He's probably at home behind the workshop. You can find him there."
- The party runs to his home. Aronious charging at the front, leg readied to kick down the door. Yin Shen kneels down as Aronious kicks. She pulls out lockpicking tools and begins to work on the locked door. Hobbins scrambles with her little paws trying to squeeze her way through gaps in the door.
- With lots of commotion and little success the door is finally opened from the inside by Polard Hinscrop, gnome store owner.
- "What are you guys doing here" He says angry with hands on hips.
- They explain how they went off on a short trip up north and are back in town and wanted to see how he was doing.
- "I waited for you guys to return and take me on an adventure, now I find you went on one without me!"
- They all apologize and say they got things mixed up. They thought he needed time to think it over.
- He forgives them because their apologies seem sincere. The party then invites him immediately to join them. He scurries in to his house where sitting on a chair at the dining room table is a fully hand-made suit of armor. The helmet is a metal pasta strainer, the shoulder pads are bent cymbals. The armor is a hodge-podge of kitchen utensils, musical instrument components, and leather straps.
- He puts everything on then disappears and reappears with a backpack twice the size as he is. "Ready to go!"
- The party decides to go to the smith. They heard that the dwarves there are able to enchant materials.
- Once there the party learns quickly that the dwarves themselves don't do it. They have connections and ship off goods for arcane modification and then re-import them to sell. They act as the middleman and its good for business to keep those secret connections.
- The party is determined to get Polard better armor as they don't believe his home made gear will be good enough. The dwarves inspect it and are in awe at the craftsmanship of it all. Sure it looks silly, but they promise that its well made and will stop a blade.
- After some bartering, Hobbins offers to teach one of the dwarven apprentices who seem most adept at magic how to use the arcane to reinforce an edge on a mundane metal sword. In trade, the dwarves will provide one steel helmet free of charge.
- Yin Shen takes this time to grab some caltrops as well.
- Aronious decided to investigate the local fletcher. He enters and sees a solitary elf. This elf while tall, is lacking in the normal elegance thought of with elves. He is heavyset with a bushy grey beard. He lazily puts feathers on an arrow shaft and hardly lifts his eyes when Aronious enters.
- Aronious sees that most bows are regular or mundane, but there is a section with ones that have intricately carved scrimshaw on bone that is reinforcing the bow. The price is steep and is one Aronious cannot afford. After a small discussion with the shop keeper, Hobbins walks in and browses with Aronious. She sees the bone reinforcment and asks Aronious. He explains that it makes it stronger and that if we can find materials, the fletcher will be willing to take only take payment for labor of crafting the bow.
- Hobbins grabs the frost giant bone she had been holding onto and puts it on the shop keepers table. "Will this work?" she questions sweetly
- "By the gods, is this... giants bone?" He holds his hands over and uses arcane magic to identify. "Frost giants bone, incredibe." His face, before stoic and uninterested was now alight with joy and determination. "Get out of my shop at once! Return in one week, I will have a bow the likes of which you have never seen."
- The party saw the flames in his eyes and did not argue. The moment they were out of the shop they heard frantic movement along with the click of locks and slamming of the wooden shutters. The fletches was now closed it would seem.
