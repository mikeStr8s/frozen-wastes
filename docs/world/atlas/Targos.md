## Connections

Targos sits on [Maer Dualdon](Maer%20Dualdon.md), two hours' walk from [Bryn Shander](Bryn%20Shander.md) and the same from [Bremen](Bremen.md), though heavy snow has obliterated the Bremen trail. Mounts and dogsleds can shorten these times by as much as 50 percent.

## The Port

Targos is where Ten-Towns keeps its sailors. [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) sent the party here when they needed passage to the Sea of Moving Ice: [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) had no real sailors and no water that led north, and what they wanted was the taverns of Targos and a captain willing to take the work.

The caravan run from Easthaven through [Bryn Shander](Bryn%20Shander.md) to Targos takes a little over nine hours.

Targos fishing crews work the ice floes of Maer Dualdon and are not uniformly welcoming. One Targos vessel jeered the party off the water during the hunt for the awakened plesiosaur, and settled only when [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) threw a gold coin into the boat and let the crew fight over it.

### Locations in Targos

- [Three Flags Sailing](Three%20Flags%20Sailing.md) — the tavern where the party found their captain. A one-eyed man with a pipe laughed them out of the room when they asked for passage onto the Sea of Moving Ice, and told them they might as well ring **Angajuk's Bell** — a bronze bell on the shore, the story goes, and a giant whale with a wrecked ship on its back, awakened once by a frost druid to carry travelers. Ring the one and the other comes.

  The laughing stopped when [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) said the voyage was meant to end the frost and that they were the ones who had killed the dragon. It was the first mate, **Dale**, who talked the captain into it. The crew muddled the price on purpose; Dale shook on thirty-five gold, with ten more due on return.
