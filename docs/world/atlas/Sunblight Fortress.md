# Sunblight Fortress

_Duergar stronghold, [Spine of the World](Spine%20of%20the%20World.md)_

[Xardorok Sunblight](../characters/Xardorok%20Sunblight.md)'s seat, and the forge where the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) was built. Sheer walls carved directly from the mountain's body, arrowslits running its face in rows and glowing faintly from within, and a narrow ice-slicked stair up the side to a single large stone door.

Its location came from a Duergar captured during the failed incursion on [Termalaine](Termalaine.md).

## The Entrance

The door had not been opened for the party. It had been **set**. A lock disengaged from somewhere within as they approached, and the crossbow bolt came through the arrowslit the moment they stepped inside.

Nine Duergar in the flanking barracks were already on their feet and already enlarging, filling the narrow hall with grey iron bulk. The space gave nothing away — too tight for [Mylbor](../characters/Mylbor%20Tarrfak.md) and [Polard](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md) to bring their considerable capacity to bear. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) made it work anyway, ricocheting chromatic orbs from one grey body to the next, while [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) plugged the corridor with the [Shield Guardian](../creatures/Shield%20Guardian.md) and [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) scattered caltrops across the threshold.

## Upper Level

- **The map room.** A relief map carved from solid stone — Ten-Towns in precise miniature, the surrounding wilderness etched down to individual ridgelines, and a channel cut between all ten settlements in a continuous loop. A dragon figurine sits on an iron pole in the channel, and a crank on the side walks it around the circuit. Someone had been plotting the route for a long time.
- **Private quarters.** A large ornate bed and a chest of gem-encrusted garments. A hatch in the chest's bottom triggered as the last item cleared it and flooded the room with **poison gas**. Hobbins held the door shut from the inside and would not let go until Yin Shen's voice, then Aronious's, then Polard's, in sequence, finally reached her. Yin Shen came through gasping and lay on the stone for some time.
- **A hidden closet** off the map room, holding several **mind flayer skulls** arranged around a chardalyn statuette. Hobbins shot the statuette apart. Yin Shen touched one of the skulls and stood perfectly still — to the party nothing happened; to her, time ran forward through days, weeks, and then ages, an accelerating pull dragging her northward while something vast and cold unwound around her. She came back the way a candle relights, all at once, and said nothing.
- **[Grandolpha Muzgardt](../characters/Grandolpha%20Muzgardt.md)'s dining room**, in the western hallway.

## Lower Level

Reached by a service elevator, past throne room guards bearing Xardorok's sigil who refused an unscheduled shift rotation without a word and without moving.

**The main forge** is a cavern swimming in heat and smoke, forty feet at the ceiling's peak and pressed down to twenty along the southern wall. At its centre stands a **pyramid forge** ten feet tall with its apex sheared off, radiating ripples of firelight and emitting a slow deep pulse from within its stone, like something breathing. A trough runs east from its base with black anvils ranged along either side.

The dragon was chained beneath an opening in the ceiling, ringed by chanting Duergar.

**The cells** held [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md).

## Afterward

Xardorok fell beside the lever he was still pulling. The dragon broke its chains and flew north.

Steel was still meeting steel beyond the walls as the party took the elevator up to the machinery room at the top, open to the sky — the sound of a fortress deciding who it belonged to. Nobody stayed to learn how it came out.

Imdra's own gear never came back out of the building.
