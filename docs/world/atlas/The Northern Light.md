# The Northern Light

*Inn*

The inn gets its name from a magic lantern that once hung above the front door.

The inn is kept by the [Shorard sisters](../characters/Cori%20and%20Allie.md).

## The Lantern

The inn is named for a **magic colour-changing lantern** that once hung above the front door. It was stolen — the most visible of a string of thefts that [Torvus](../characters/Torvus.md) spent a long time failing to solve, and that [Cori](../characters/Cori%20and%20Allie.md) would not let him hear the end of.

The thieves were [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md). Allie found the same shrinking footprints in the snow behind the storeroom that the party had seen in an Easthaven jail cell.

The party recovered the lantern from the Duergar stronghold at [Kelvin's Cairn](Kelvin's%20Cairn.md) and returned it, along with a bound prisoner and the beginnings of a much larger story.
