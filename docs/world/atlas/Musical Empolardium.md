# Musical Empolardium

_Instrument shop, [Easthaven](Easthaven.md)_

[Polard Hinscrop](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md)'s rebuilt music shop, successor to **Strings and Sundries** and **Drums Emporium** both — see the [Music Shop Conspiracy](../organizations/Music%20Shop%20Conspiracy.md) for how it got that way.

The old shop and the whole of its lane were destroyed when the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md)'s radiant breath went straight down it. Polard knelt in the rubble and wept, then produced the insurance pamphlet the party had given him and showed them the last unchecked box.

> "I never got it notarized."

A week later the plot was twice its old size, Easthaven's thanks for his part in the defense — a tent, a sign, a freshly built workbench, and no merchandise of any kind.

## The Shop Now

Bigger than the old one and about half finished. Polard has hired an assistant named **[Samantha](../characters/Samantha.md)** to work the counter, and means to install a **forge** and train with the Easthaven blacksmith so he can supply the party's gear himself.

The party offered a better name for the place. It was a pun. Polard did not need to think about it.

> "Samantha, we need to change the sign immediately."

## The Sign

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) went up on the roof to help with the sign, and tried to unhook it to prove she was helping.

The sign won. It took her off the roof with it and into a snowbank.

Polard charged her one gold for the breakage, and winked at [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md).
