# Auril's Island

_Island in the [Sea of Moving Ice](Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md)_

[Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s own island, marked on a map by [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) at a point the Targos captain swore held no land at all. He set a course for it anyway.

It is the current objective. The **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)** is kept here, and in it an incantation that can split the [Reghed Glacier](Reghed%20Glacier.md) and open [Ythryn](Ythryn.md).

## Approach

The cold came on before the island did — biting, and wrong for the latitude. Then fog. The ship came out of it under **cliffs of ice fifty feet high**, with masts standing up out of the water around them and the ships they belonged to somewhere underneath.

Ahead was a dock carved from ice, shattered, and **built for giants**.

The crew anchored offshore and refused to land. They would wait one day; the second cost fifty gold, haggled down by first mate Dale to the ten already owed on return plus one magic item from whatever the party found ashore.

## Soppo's Tour

An ice mephit named **[Soppo](../characters/Soppo.md)** was waiting on the dock, waving and jabbering in Auran. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) cast _comprehend languages_ as a ritual and did the talking from there.

Soppo offered to guide them. There is, by its account, plenty to see:

- **Shipwrecks**, full of goods
- **A recent wreck** with one survivor, who made it as far as the shore and froze there
- **A field of ice sculptures**
- **[Grimskalle](Grimskalle.md)**, at the centre of the island, where the lady's book is kept, and where it is dangerous

> "Soppo hasn't had visitors in a while. Again, unless you count the one who crawled ashore, though she didn't last."

Asked about [Professor Skant](../artifacts/Professor%20Skant.md), Soppo did not know the orb. Asked about the Codicil of White, it looked shocked.

The tour runs fourteen hours, with Grimskalle at the end of it. The party accepted. [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) kept her guard up.
