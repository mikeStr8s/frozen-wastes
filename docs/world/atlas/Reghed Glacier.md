# Reghed Glacier

_Glacier_

The great glacier of [Icewind Dale](Icewind%20Dale.md), and the tomb of the Netherese city of [Ythryn](Ythryn.md).

[Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s magic guards its ice. [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) has evidently tried and failed to get through it — fire will not open it, and neither will a pick. What will is an incantation in the **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)**, kept on [Auril's Island](Auril's%20Island.md).

Nothing else about the glacier has entered the record yet. The party has not been there.
