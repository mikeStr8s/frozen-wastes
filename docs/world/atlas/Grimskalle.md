# Grimskalle

_Fortress at the centre of [Auril's Island](Auril's%20Island.md)_

Where [Auril](../deities/Auril.md) keeps the **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)**.

Everything known about it so far comes from one source: the ice mephit [Soppo](../characters/Soppo.md), who looked shocked when [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) asked after the book and called it _the lady's book_.

It lies at the centre of the island, at the end of a fourteen-hour tour of shipwrecks, a frozen corpse, and a field of ice sculptures.

Soppo says it is **dangerous**.

The party is on its way.
