# Ythryn

_Buried Netherese city_

A city of the [Netherese Empire](../lore/The%20Netherese%20Empire.md) lying buried in the [Reghed Glacier](Reghed%20Glacier.md). It is what [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) has been doing in [Icewind Dale](Icewind%20Dale.md) all along — the thing he never mentioned while traveling with the party as "Gustavo."

He told them only after the ceremorphs' ship left the valley, standing in the snow as a blue translucent projection with the landscape visible through him.

## The Seal

[Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s magic guards the ice over the city. **Fire will not open it, and neither will a pick.**

A book will. On [Auril's Island](Auril's%20Island.md), out in the [Sea of Moving Ice](Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md), there is a tome called the **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)**, and in it an incantation that can split the glacier.

## The Contract

Dzaan wants two things:

1. The Codicil of White, retrieved from the island
2. **[Professor Skant](../artifacts/Professor%20Skant.md)**, his professor orb and an expert on the Netherese Empire, stolen from him

The payoff is the city itself, and whatever magic is still lying around in it.

The party took the job. He marked the island on their map.
