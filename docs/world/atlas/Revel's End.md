# Revel's End

_Prison_

A prison in the far north, and the only port [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) knew of close to [Auril's Island](Auril's%20Island.md).

He offered it as the obvious route when he set the party on the [Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md). The party pointed out two problems: it is a prison, and there is an [ancient white dragon](../creatures/Ancient%20White%20Dragon.md) in the way.

They asked in [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) instead, and ended up chartering out of [Targos](Targos.md).

Nothing further is known. The party has not been there.
