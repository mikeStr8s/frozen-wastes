## Heraldry

A snowflake at the top center of a steel-gray field, above a horizontal brown field that forms the left-hand base, and a dark blue field that forms the right-hand base; where they meet is a thin white tower. The brown field represents the Eastway, the tower represents the town, and the blue field is Lac Dinneshere.

## Connections

The Eastway stretches between Easthaven and Bryn Shander. Snow-covered paths branching off this road lead to Caer-Dineval and Goodmead. Travel times in the Overland Travel from Easthaven table assume that characters are on foot; mounts and dogsleds can shorten these times by as much as 50 percent.

## The Dragon Attack

Easthaven was struck twice. The first incursion was hard, fast, and purposeful: the great front gate was blackened by fire and split by a force that had not bothered to find a seam, and [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) was taken prisoner. The party saw the damage half-repaired in the cold as they rode past toward the Spine of the World.

The second was the Chardalyn Dragon. Easthaven had eight hours' warning and spent it patching the walls with packed ice and snow and massing two hundred militia — a hundred and fifty in uniform and fifty who had simply turned up — alongside a hundred and fifty evacuees from Dougan's Hole and Goodmead.

Radiant breath shattered a section of wall and took a part of the vanguard with it. By the time the dragon burst, the line had thinned to around eighty. The blast left a crater and a missing section of wall, and nothing in the crater but chardalyn fragments. On [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md)'s warning about what prolonged contact with the material does to a mind, Imdra put a task force on burying it and militia on keeping the townsfolk away.

A whole district burned. [Polard Hinscrop](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md)'s lane took the breath straight down it and not one building was left standing.
