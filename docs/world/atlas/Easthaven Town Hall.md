# Easthaven Town Hall

_Civic building, [Easthaven](Easthaven.md)_

Clerk's offices at the front, a grand promenade with stairs to the second floor, and a dungeon level below holding cells. The party has done more business here than anywhere else in Ten-Towns.

At the centre of the promenade stands a **ship's figurehead carved into the shape of a demon**. A large black [chardalyn](../lore/Chardalyn.md) crystal once jutted from its chest.

## The Theft

The party found the doors unlocked and slightly askew one dark evening, the entryway pitch black, no lanterns lit and no guards posted. They were, as [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) worked out partway through, trespassing in a closed building — which raised the question of why the doors were unlocked and damaged.

The figurehead's chest gaped empty, black crystalline shards around the cavity and splinters on the floor below. Three guards were found bound, gagged and unconscious in a lower room, with the far wall caved in and a crude tunnel leading out to the frozen streets.

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) followed a trail of crystal fragments into a corner and collided with something unseen. The invisibility shattered, and a [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md) in furs and orange armour with a clinking sack over his shoulder shoved her back and bolted.

They ran him down in the street and Hobbins pinned his hand to the floor with her dagger.

## The Cell

He admitted his people were stockpiling chardalyn and would say no more.

He did not stay. Yin Shen returned to the basement to find the dust disturbed, clear signs of struggle, and **footprints leading toward the door, each one progressively smaller than the last**, vanishing entirely at the threshold.

That detail — recognised again in the snow behind an inn in [Caer-Konig](Caer-Konig.md) — is what unravelled the conspiracy.

## Authority

This is where [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) read [Torvus](../characters/Torvus.md)' writ and added her own endorsement in wax, making the party agents of Easthaven in all but formal title.

It is also where a later prisoner drove hidden chardalyn shards into his own flesh in confinement, and had to be put down.

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) kicked the door in on her return from the north, shouting Imdra's name. Imdra was back on duty in a plain standard uniform.
