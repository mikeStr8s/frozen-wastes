# The Goblin Stronghold

_Ruin, [Spine of the World](Spine%20of%20the%20World.md)_

A goblin settlement built into an interior clearing at the end of a ravine carved into the mountain rock — watchtowers, timber walls, and the whole crude architecture of a community dug in and making itself permanent. The guards paid far more attention to the braziers beside them than to the perimeter.

The [Shield Guardian](../creatures/Shield%20Guardian.md) hung at its centre from rope and timber like a puppet with its strings cut. That is what brought the party, following the pull of [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s amulet.

## The Chief

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) walked up to the gates as though she had every right to be there, took an arrow in the ground at her feet, and announced they were expected. The gates opened to reveal a larger goblin astride a worg.

> "Are you the emissaries?"

She agreed that they were.

The chief turned out to be [Spellix Romwood](../characters/Spellix%20Romwood.md), a gnome merchant in a goblin mask who had been trying to free his captured caravan and had stumbled into the role of chief instead. He was certain several of the elders had seen through him.

[Polard Hinscrop](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md) had to be disguised as a dwarf on the way in — goblins and gnomes being natural enemies, and a dwarf being better odds.

## The Fire

The plan was to wait for dusk and move on the Guardian in the dark. It went poorly from the moment the construct activated.

Every door, shutter, and dark gap in the timber walls disgorged goblins at once — not a patrol, not a garrison, a horde, with arrows coming not in volleys but in a constant hissing sheet. The **worgs** hit hardest, coordinated in a way that felt almost deliberate.

It was not a battle the party could win. [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) made the call, secured a rope and grappling hook at the battlements, and held the exit with a blade until everyone was down it.

Before that, [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) looked back at the stronghold.

Then she set it on fire.

As the last of the party hit the ground outside, the shouts within split — half still howling pursuit, half now screaming at the fire spreading hungrily through the timber buildings.

Behind them, somewhere in the mountain, the stronghold burned.
