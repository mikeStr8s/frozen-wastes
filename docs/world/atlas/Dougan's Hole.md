The smallest and southernmost of Ten-Towns, on the shore of Redwaters. [Torg's Trading Company](../organizations/Torg's%20Trading%20Company.md) travel vouchers explicitly do not service it.

## The Evacuation

Dougan's Hole was first on the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md)'s route — the channel cut through the stone map in [Sunblight Fortress](Sunblight%20Fortress.md) began here and ran north through [Goodmead](Goodmead.md) to [Easthaven](Easthaven.md). The party worked out eight hours for the dragon, and eight hours for themselves in a straight line to Easthaven.

That settled the question of rest. It did not settle the question of the two small towns in between.

Goodmead had no speaker. Dougan's Hole had [Edgra Dermoot](../characters/Edgra%20Dermoot.md), an old trapper woman. So it would be Edgra: Dougan's Hole would run for Easthaven and gather up Goodmead on the way.

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) cast _sending_ from Aronious's arms as he ran for the dogsled. The answer came back curt.

> Dougan's Hole was leaving its houses where they stood.

Around a hundred and fifty people from the two towns reached Easthaven's gate ahead of the dragon, carrying what they could. The dragon's flight bent away toward Dougan's Hole as the party's sled outran it north.
