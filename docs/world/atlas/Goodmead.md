## The Empty Seat

Goodmead has no speaker. The last one died in a verbeeg incursion, and the seat has sat empty since while a handful of hopefuls reach for it — [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md)'s father among them.

That vacancy mattered more than anyone expected. When the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) broke out of [Sunblight Fortress](Sunblight%20Fortress.md) and began following the route carved into Xardorok's stone map — [Dougan's Hole](Dougan's%20Hole.md), then Goodmead, then [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) — there was no one in Goodmead to send a warning to.

The party sent it to [Edgra Dermoot](../characters/Edgra%20Dermoot.md) of Dougan's Hole instead, with instructions to gather Goodmead's people on the way past. Around a hundred and fifty people from the two towns made Easthaven's gate ahead of the dragon.

Smoke stood in the sky from the direction of Goodmead as the party made their report on the wall.
