# The Magikal Morgue

_Magical curiosity shop, [Goodmead](Goodmead.md)_

The Hobbins family's shop, and [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md)'s childhood home.

Despite the name, the business is perfectly legitimate — if a little unsettling to first-time visitors. The family buys magical items recovered from the frozen wastes, whether salvaged from the remains of unlucky adventurers or sold on by the rare survivors who make it back alive. Every item is carefully catalogued, restored where restoration is possible, and sold to collectors, travellers, and aspiring adventurers.

It has made the Hobbins family one of the more prosperous households in Goodmead.

## The Proprietors

- **Logely Hobbins** — mother. Handles the books and the negotiations with sharp precision.
- **Drumly Hobbins** — father. An uncanny eye for an authentic magical relic, and one of the hopefuls reaching for Goodmead's empty speaker's seat.

Both love their daughter and consider her career an unnecessary gamble. In their view, adventurers are future customers — or else future inventory. They spent years watching ambitious heroes throw away promising futures chasing treasure and return broke, or not return at all. Magic is valuable and knowledge is valuable, but risking your life for either is foolish when you could study and trade them instead.

They sent [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) to the magical academies of Waterdeep expecting a scholar, an enchanter, or a merchant capable of expanding the business far beyond Icewind Dale. Every letter since has politely encouraged her to come to her senses and return to it.

## What Hobbins Intends

She grew up surrounded by enchanted trinkets, cursed curiosities, and stories of impossible adventures, and she loved all of it.

One day she means to walk back through the door carrying artifacts no merchant could ever have purchased, because nobody else had ever found them.
