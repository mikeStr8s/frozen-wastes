# Sea of Moving Ice

_Frozen sea north of Icewind Dale_

A frozen expanse broken by pressure ridges and deep blue fissures, stretching away under a sky that seems to press down rather than lift. It announces itself gradually and then all at once: the ground grows harder, the air sharper, and the horizon shows the grey-white suggestion of pack ice.

## The Shipwreck

One of the [ancient white dragon](../creatures/Ancient%20White%20Dragon.md)'s lairs. The hull rises from the ice at a list, half-consumed by the freeze, every timber grey and salt-stripped, and opened by something that had simply decided a wall was an inconvenience.

Inside: a frost troll, four kobolds in a barricaded room, and the dragon's hoard glimmering under the ice.

[Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md)'s cave is within running distance of it, and the party reached it ahead of a rain of falling ice spears.

## Sailing North

There is no port on this sea that Ten-Towns can offer. [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) has no real sailors and no water that leads here; [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) sent the party to the taverns of [Targos](Targos.md) for a captain instead. The nearest port [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) knew of is **Revel's End**, which is a prison, and the white dragon lies in the way regardless.

The crossing from Targos runs a day of breaking ice upriver and two days at sea. Legend offers an alternative: **[Angajuk's Bell](../characters/Angajuk.md)**, a bronze bell on the shore that is said to summon a giant whale with a wrecked ship on its back, awakened once by a frost druid to carry travelers.

## Hazards

The cold comes on wrong before the ice does — biting, and too hard for the latitude, as the Targos captain observed. Then fog, and a great deal of it.

Under cliffs of ice fifty feet high, **masts stand up out of the water** with the ships they belong to somewhere underneath. The crew that carried the party refused to land and would wait only one day without renegotiating.

> "What do you think sunk these ships? I don't see them stuck on any rocks."
