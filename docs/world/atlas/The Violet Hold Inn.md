# The Violet Hold Inn

_Unverified — an open lead_

A name on a violet slip of paper, pressed into the party's hands by a dark-robed stranger who slipped out of the crowd on a frozen [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) street.

> "The Violet Hold Inn may have what you seek. They specialize in moving objects of power."

He tried to vanish immediately. [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) and [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) stopped him and pulled his hood down, and found only a hunched elderly courier who knew just enough to be suspicious and not enough to be useful.

The party let him go with more questions than answers.

The lead came while they were trying to work out what the **temporal stasis chest** from [The Black Spire](The%20Black%20Spire.md) could do, having just been offered scrap value for it by a dwarven smith who thought it would make a breastplate or two.

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    The party has never gone to the Violet Hold Inn. Its location is unknown, nobody has confirmed it exists, and nobody has explained how a stranger knew what they were carrying.
