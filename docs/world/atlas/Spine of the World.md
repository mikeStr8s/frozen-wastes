# Spine of the World

_Mountain range_

The great range along the southern edge of [Icewind Dale](Icewind%20Dale.md), separating the Dale from the lands below. Its passes are the only land route in or out, and they are not safe ones — [Spellix Romwood](../characters/Spellix%20Romwood.md)'s caravan was ambushed by goblins in the pass to the west, and [The Goblin Stronghold](The%20Goblin%20Stronghold.md) sits in a ravine carved into the rock nearby.

## The Stronghold in the Spine

Deep in these mountains, [Xardorok Sunblight](../characters/Xardorok%20Sunblight.md) built [Sunblight Fortress](Sunblight%20Fortress.md) and the forge that made the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md).

Its location came out of a Duergar captured during the failed incursion on [Termalaine](Termalaine.md), and reached the party through Speaker [Oarus Masthew](../characters/Oarus%20Masthew.md) in the same breath as the news that [Easthaven](Easthaven.md) had been hit and [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) taken.

[Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) guided the party up with a ranger's instinct, reading the terrain and steering them clear of the worst of it — the hidden drops, the unstable slopes, the places where the snow moved. They climbed until the foothills gave way to raw stone and the fortress rose above them without warning.
