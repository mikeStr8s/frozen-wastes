# Kelvin's Cairn

_Mountain_

The lone mountain of [Icewind Dale](Icewind%20Dale.md), rising from the tundra north of the three lakes. Bitter winds blow down off it and across [Lac Dinneshere](Lac%20Dinneshere.md), and dwarven mines work its stone.

## The Iron Shipment

A shipment of iron bound for [Blackiron Blades](Blackiron%20Blades.md) of [Bryn Shander](Bryn%20Shander.md) was lost on the mountain when the dwarves carrying it down from the mines were attacked by a **yeti**. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) heard about it over the bar at [The Wet Trout](The%20Wet%20Trout.md). The smiths offered gold for its return, and friendship besides — which for a dwarf is often worth more.

The load has never been recovered.

## The Duergar Stronghold

Following footprints and dogsled tracks northeast out of [Caer-Konig](Caer-Konig.md), the party found a stronghold carved directly into the stone at the mountain's base — the first hard evidence that the Duergar conspiracy was real.

Inside they found a guard outpost with no external entrance, a trapdoor and tunnel running under the wall, a larger-than-normal dogsled, a **zombie ogre** caged beside the goats stolen from Caer-Konig, a spiked corridor trap, holding cells where denizens of Ten-Towns had been transformed into **fungal monsters**, and the magic lantern taken from [The Northern Light](The%20Northern%20Light.md).

A Duergar in finery rather than armor was pinned in the spike trap and interrogated. In Dwarvish, he bellowed:

> My father is more powerful than any of you filthy creatures could imagine! You don't understand the glory of it, the power found in the ice. None of you do. But you will learn soon enough when my father unleashes his terror upon Ten-Towns! Your doom soars on dragon's wings!

A crumpled letter found in his room pointed to the next base:

> _Brother,_
> _You will find me on the frozen ferry in Easthaven. From this new base, the search for chardalyn continues. Long may our father reign over this dark land!_
> _— Durth_

The father was Xardorok Sunblight. The dragon was real.

## The Ravine

On the approach, in a ravine short of the mountain, the party found the back half of a polar bear taken in a single bite — and then a snowdrift that shifted and fell away to reveal the head and neck of an **ancient white dragon**, a saddle peeking from the snow with a robed figure strapped into it, unmoving. They crept past without waking it. On the return trip it had been re-buried by fresh drifts.
