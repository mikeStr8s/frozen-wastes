# The Black Spire

_Netherese ruin — also called Dzaan's tower_

A structure jutting roughly twenty feet out of the frozen earth in the wilderness northeast of [Caer-Konig](Caer-Konig.md), its walls perfectly smooth and deeply black. Not dark — _black_, in the way that swallows the grey light of an overcast sky rather than reflecting it. No snow clings to its surface.

The party reached it from a map [Randy Grailden](../characters/Randy%20Grailden.md) sold [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) for twenty gold, navigating by having the sled dogs track the scent of [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s fireball scroll across an ocean of featureless white. Scouts dispatched by [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) had confirmed only that something was out there, and that it was not natural.

## Upside Down

Something turned the spire over. The first room inside is **inverted**: everything that once rested on the floor now dangles from what the party stood on, or lies broken where it fell, while anything bolted down remains fixed to what is now the ceiling.

Yin Shen gathered texts on the [Netherese empire](../lore/The%20Netherese%20Empire.md) from the fallen shelves, written in hands long dead.

## The Laboratory

A hole in the floor leads down to a wizard's laboratory — shattered glass across every surface, strange liquids pooled and mingled in colours that don't have names, equipment broken and scattered, experiments interrupted by whatever catastrophe inverted the place.

A chest bolted to what had been the floor hangs from the ceiling here. Yin Shen picked its lock at an uncomfortable angle and caught all four falling phials before one could shatter: **four potions of resistance**.

A ruined bedchamber nearby holds the corpse of a long-dead wizard and an **invisible, locked chest** that keeps its contents in temporal stasis — airtight, watertight, fixed at a constant temperature. The leather-bound book inside crumbled to ash the instant it left, experiencing two thousand years in moments. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) kept the chest and has since ridden out a radiant catastrophe inside it.

## The Occupants

The spire was not empty. **Black slimes** with acid-soaked pseudopods nearly killed Hobbins on the level below.

Deeper still was [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s **simulacrum**, working over parchment with an undead attendant named **[Krintas](../characters/Krintas.md)**, muttering about failed experiments and a missing **lifespark**.

> "Dammit — all experiments failed. I need a lifespark."

He warned them, before they went down: _"Just a warning before going further, there are a few experiments below. Do avoid using acid, cold, or lightning while down there!"_

## The Netherese Machine

At the lowest level is a chamber of runes etched across the walls and apparatus, cracked deeply through the stone and flickering with instability. A central platform and a pedestal.

Its first use made the simulacrum **fully corporeal**. The vortex destabilised, radiant energy erupted in a violent flash, Yin Shen and [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) were struck by bolts, and Krintas took the worst of it — hurled into a wall, flesh scorched and smouldering.

Its second use turned a detailed _minor illusion_ of a blowgun with poisoned and sleep-laced darts into **a real blowgun and real darts**, clattering onto the stone floor without any radiant backlash at all.

> "One use left, maybe," Hobbins warned.

The party chose to save it.

The machine has one use remaining, the damage is worsening, and nobody has yet decided what it is for.
