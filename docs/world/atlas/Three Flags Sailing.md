# Three Flags Sailing

_Tavern, [Targos](Targos.md)_

The tavern where ships are chartered out of Targos, and where the party found the only crew willing to sail onto the [Sea of Moving Ice](Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md).

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) looked the room over and settled on a large man with one eye and a pipe. A captain. She asked him for passage.

**The whole table laughed.** She might as well ring [Angajuk](../characters/Angajuk.md)'s Bell, he told her — a bronze bell on the shore, and a giant whale with a wrecked ship on its back, awakened once by a frost druid to carry travelers. Ring the one and the other comes.

Then Yin Shen said the voyage was meant to end the frost, and that they were the ones who had killed the dragon.

The laughing stopped. The captain turned to his crew, and it was his first mate [Dale](../characters/Dale.md) who talked him into it.

The crew muddled the price on purpose. Dale shook on **thirty-five gold**, paid before sailing — with ten more, the quartermaster mentioned at the dock, due on return.
