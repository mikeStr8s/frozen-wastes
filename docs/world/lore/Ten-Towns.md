# Ten-Towns

_The settlements of Icewind Dale_

Ten independent towns scattered around the three lakes of [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md). Each has its own **Speaker**; no single ruler governs them all. [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) asked after the leader of all Ten-Towns early on and was told plainly that there isn't one.

That absence of central authority is the political problem running under the whole of the party's work. Warning Ten-Towns about the Duergar meant warning ten separate people, one at a time, and hoping each moved.

## The Party's Standing

The party holds two written authorities, both earned rather than granted:

- A writ from [Torvus](../characters/Torvus.md) **deputizing them as agents of [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md)**, reporting directly to the town Speaker, signed and sealed with his signet.
- An endorsement added by [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) on the same document stating that while not formally deputized, they are **working under official business of Captain Imdra and [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md)**.

That paper opened the door at [Termalaine](../atlas/Termalaine.md) and got [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) onto the wall during the dragon's assault.

## Mobilisation

Imdra sent notices to every town speaker and warned it would take time, as not everyone moved as quickly as she did. She was right on both counts.

- **Termalaine** mobilised immediately. [Oarus Masthew](../characters/Oarus%20Masthew.md) drilled every able-bodied person who could hold a spear in the street, repelled its own Duergar incursion, and captured the prisoner who gave up [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md).
- By the time the party returned from the black spire, **three more towns** had begun reinforcing militia, putting half of Ten-Towns in some state of readiness.
- **Easthaven** was hit anyway — hard, fast, and with purpose — and Imdra was taken.
