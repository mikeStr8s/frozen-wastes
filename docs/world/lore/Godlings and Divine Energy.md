# Godlings and Divine Energy

_Lore_

[Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md) holds a population of **awakened beasts** — creatures granted intelligence, power, and a domain of their own. They call themselves godlings, and they keep a balance between them.

A godling is recognisable by the faint blue glow or blue wisps rising from its form, and by the collar or band many of them wear. The party found a chamber of ancient stone pillars beneath the cliffs of [Lac Dinneshere](../atlas/Lac%20Dinneshere.md) painted floor-to-ceiling with images of beasts — wolves, bears, eagles — uncommonly large and surrounded by smaller kin, each great creature collared and haloed in blue light.

## How It Works

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) researched this properly in the records room at [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md), in a book called **_Nature and the Divine_**:

> Godlings act as **conduits**. Should the vessel for divine energy be destroyed, the energy disperses or finds a new host.

She came out of it with an idea she has not yet acted on — that divine energy might be deliberately funnelled into [Lupine](../characters/Lupine.md).

The same research let her end the standoff on [Maer Dualdon](../atlas/Maer%20Dualdon.md) without a fight. The awakened plesiosaur believed that if it stopped tormenting Bremen the spell would break and it would become a mindless beast again. Hobbins convinced it that **the awakening is permanent** — nothing bound it to that place or compelled it to obey. It descended into the water and shot off away from the town.

## Awakening as a Weapon

Not all awakenings are gifts. **[Ravison](../characters/Ravison.md)**, a frost druid of [Auril](../deities/Auril.md), has awakened multiple animals across Ten-Towns and set them against the people — the Maer Dualdon plesiosaur was commanded to make Bremen suffer, and it did.

The legend of **[Angajuk](../characters/Angajuk.md)**, the whale with a wrecked ship on its back, says a frost druid awakened it too, to carry travelers.

## Boons

A godling can grant power deliberately. After the party killed Skree, [Lupine](../characters/Lupine.md) rose to his full height, had them hold their weapons aloft, and exhaled frozen air that settled on the steel and wood as ice crystals before fading into them. Both [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) and [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) carry the result as **Lupine's Boon**: +1 to hit, +1 to damage.
