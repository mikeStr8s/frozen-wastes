# The Rime

_The endless winter_

[Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s unending night over [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md). The sun does not properly rise. The snow does not stop. Every town of Ten-Towns pays a **sacrifice to Auril** to survive it, and the kind varies by town — food, warmth, mead, and in three of them, human.

The party saw its maintenance first-hand on a caravan north to [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md): the temperature dropping sharply, the stars going out, the world sucked into total darkness, and Auril herself streaking across the black casting terrible magics to keep it going relentlessly, without mercy or end.

## What It Costs

The Rime is not only cold. It is an economy collapsing in slow motion.

> "Times is rough with the winter's Rime shuttering travel, trade, and the joy in our hearts. This is all we have left." — [Cori](../characters/Cori%20and%20Allie.md), handing over three gold and six silver, the whole of the inn's savings

The Rime fights back against those who travel against it. Pushing northeast out of Caer-Konig toward the Duergar stronghold, the wind and snow picked up dramatically and cut visibility to nothing.

## Who Opposes It

- **[Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md)**, verbeeg servant of [Sylvanas](../deities/Sylvanas.md) — an endless, unnatural winter pressed against the grain of the world itself is her concern, and anything that moves against Auril can count on her.
- **[Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)** — his interest in [Ythryn](../atlas/Ythryn.md) runs through the [Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md), and the incantation in it that can split Auril's ice.
- **The party** — the voyage north to [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md) was sold to a Targos captain on exactly this basis: the voyage was meant to end the frost.

## The Irony

The weapon that could have burned the Rime away was in the hands of the people least interested in doing it.

> _"Bright enough to BURN away the Rime — to burn us all if you do... not... stop..."_ — [Hethyl Arkorran](../characters/Hethyl%20Arkorran.md)

The party stopped it. The Rime continues.
