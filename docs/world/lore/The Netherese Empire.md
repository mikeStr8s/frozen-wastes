# The Netherese Empire

_Ancient civilization_

A dead empire of enormous arcane reach, roughly two thousand years gone, whose ruins are scattered under the ice of [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md). What it left behind is beyond modern understanding — [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md), asked to read the runes on a Netherese device, could confirm only that they were ancient Netherese and leave it there.

## The Black Spire

The party's first contact was [The Black Spire](../atlas/The%20Black%20Spire.md), a structure of perfectly smooth, deeply black stone jutting twenty feet out of the frozen earth, swallowing the grey light rather than reflecting it, with no snow clinging to its surface. Something had turned the whole thing upside down.

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) gathered texts on the Netherese empire from its overturned shelves, written in hands long dead, and studied them on the road whenever there was light. She still carries a **book of Netheril**.

At the bottom was a functioning Netherese machine — see [The Netherese Machine](../atlas/The%20Black%20Spire.md). It made [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s simulacrum fully corporeal, and turned a detailed _minor illusion_ of a blowgun into a real one.

## Temporal Stasis

An invisible, locked chest found in a ruined bedchamber of the spire held contents in **temporal stasis** — airtight, watertight, fixed at a constant temperature. The leather-bound book inside crumbled instantly to ash the moment Yin Shen lifted it out, experiencing roughly two thousand years of age in moments.

Hobbins kept the chest. She has ridden out at least one radiant catastrophe inside it.

## Ythryn

The prize is [Ythryn](../atlas/Ythryn.md), a Netherese city buried in the [Reghed Glacier](../atlas/Reghed%20Glacier.md). [Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s magic guards the ice; neither fire nor pick will open it. The **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)** holds an incantation that will.

[Professor Skant](../artifacts/Professor%20Skant.md), Dzaan's stolen professor orb, is an expert on the Netherese Empire. Recovering it is part of the same contract.

The payoff, as Dzaan put it, is the city itself — and whatever magic is still lying around in it.
