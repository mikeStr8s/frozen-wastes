# Chardalyn

_Material_

A black crystal that holds magic — and holds something else besides. Chardalyn is the substance the entire Duergar conspiracy was built to gather, and the material the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) was made from.

## What It Does to People

Prolonged contact causes a kind of **necrosis of the mind**. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) warned [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) of it directly after the dragon fell, and told her the fragments in the crater should be buried rather than handled.

He had evidence. A captured Duergar held in Easthaven had hidden chardalyn shards on his person and drove them into his own flesh in confinement. Whatever demonic force lives in the crystal devoured what remained of his mind. By the time the guards intervened he had become something feral and unrecognizable, and they put him down.

## The Thefts

The Duergar were stockpiling it. The prisoner taken at the Easthaven town hall admitted that much and would say no more — and when [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) leaned in and asked about a mechanical dragon, his eyes darted, sweat beaded, and he hissed through his teeth:

> "You'll get no more from me."

Known thefts and sources:

- **Easthaven town hall** — a large black crystal torn from the chest of a demon-headed ship's figurehead in the promenade, leaving black crystalline shards around the cavity and splinters on the floor below.
- **The Easthaven ferry** — named in [Durth](../characters/Durth.md)'s letter as a base from which "the search for chardalyn continues."
- A chardalyn **statuette** in a hidden closet at [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md), ringed by mind flayer skulls. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) put a crossbow bolt through it without preamble.

## The Prophecy

[Hethyl Arkorran](../characters/Hethyl%20Arkorran.md) saw what it was being gathered for, and died saying so:

> _"Hidden away, buried in the mountains, lies a fortress, a villain, a crystal machine with a dragon's heart! O, mighty wings beating and breath like the sun! Bright enough to BURN away the Rime — to burn us all if you do... not... stop..."_

## Research

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) carries a **chardalyn notebook** and studied the material whenever there was light to read by. [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s simulacrum traded everything he knew about chardalyn as part of the price for being made corporeal.

When the dragon burst at the walls of Easthaven, it shattered into flecks too small to hold its magic. There was nothing in the crater but fragments.
