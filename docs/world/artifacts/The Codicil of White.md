# The Codicil of White

_Tome — objective_

A book kept on [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md), out in the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md). [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) named it and sent the party after it.

It holds an **incantation that can split the [Reghed Glacier](../atlas/Reghed%20Glacier.md)** and open the buried Netherese city of [Ythryn](../atlas/Ythryn.md). [Auril](../deities/Auril.md)'s magic guards that ice; fire will not open it, and neither will a pick.

The ice mephit [Soppo](../characters/Soppo.md) looked shocked when [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) asked about it, and called it **the lady's book**. It is kept at [Grimskalle](../atlas/Grimskalle.md), at the centre of the island, where Soppo says it is dangerous.

Not yet recovered.
