# Yin Shen's Kimono

_Celestial garment — lost_

The outfit [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) wore into battle as a goddess. While she had it on, **no one was able to hear her coming.**

Worn with the [Black Lotus Sword](Black%20Lotus%20Sword.md), it made her the silent half of a pair — her twin [Shuāng](../characters/Shuāng.md) loud and armored at the front, Yin Shen arriving from behind without a sound.

## Status

**Unrecovered.**

Taken from her by [Tyr](../deities/Tyr.md) at her judgement and **scattered across [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md)** with the rest of her regalia.

!!! note "Name"
    Yin Shen's player refers to it only as her "special kimono outfit" and "kimono armor." The title of this article is a placeholder until it has a proper name.
