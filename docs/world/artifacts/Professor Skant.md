# Professor Skant

_Professor orb — objective_

[Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s professor orb, and **an expert on the [Netherese Empire](../lore/The%20Netherese%20Empire.md)**. It was stolen from him.

He wants it back, and asked for its recovery in the same breath as the [Codicil of White](The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md) when he set the party on [Ythryn](../atlas/Ythryn.md).

The ice mephit [Soppo](../characters/Soppo.md), asked about the orb on the dock at [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md), did not know it.

Whereabouts unknown. Not yet recovered.
