# Off-putting Effigy

*Wonderous Item, Uncommon*

Puts off an aura of unease that can cloud even the most insightful.

Insight checks within 30ft of this totem are done with disadvantage.

## Origin

Recovered from a shadowed alcove deep in [Skree](../characters/Skree.md)'s cave, southeast of [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), radiating the same foul energy that had rotted the godling from the inside. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) wrapped it in cloth to dull its influence before carrying it out.

The corruption was not Skree's own doing. Whatever placed the totem there has not been found.
