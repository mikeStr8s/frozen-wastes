# Black Lotus Sword

_Celestial blade — lost_

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md)'s sword from her days as a goddess of trickery. **A blade as dark as night, and silent like the grave.**

She fought with it in the shadows, coming in behind an enemy line while her twin [Shuāng](../characters/Shuāng.md) held its attention from the front. Paired with her [kimono](Yin%20Shen's%20Kimono.md), nobody heard her coming — and by the time they did, it was already too late.

## Status

**Unrecovered.**

[Tyr](../deities/Tyr.md) stripped it from her when he delivered judgement, along with her title, her magic, her immortality, and her kimono. He did not destroy any of it.

He **scattered her possessions across [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md)**.

Finding them again is one of the two things Yin Shen has been working toward since the day she landed in the snow.
