# Chwinga

_Tiny Elemental, Neutral_

A chwinga is a tiny elemental spirit that lives in plants, rocks, and rivers far from civilization. Those found in cold climates also live in ice and snow. Painfully shy, chwingas prefer to move about unseen.

Chwingas resemble 6-inch-tall animated dolls with mask-like faces, spindly limbs, and wild hair. Their appearance is sometimes foreshadowed by gentle breezes, the sweet smell of flowers, dancing fireflies, or snowflakes. They don't have names and cannot speak.

## Party Interactions

The party first saw one in a moonlit glade with [Sephek Kaltro](../characters/Sephek%20Kaltro.md)'s blade poised above it. They killed him. [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) paid them thirty gold to bring him one for study; they danced, sang, and played ring-around-the-rosy in the snow to coax one out, and nothing came.

The chwingas came in their own time instead.

### The Charms

On a night watch in the tundra, one jabbed [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) sharply in the ribs — tiny, bright-eyed, trembling with barely contained delight at its own audacity. When he did not shout or swat at it, it dissolved into a fit of tinkling laughter, and the two of them sat together in the cold for a while.

Then it darted behind a rock and came back carrying a **snowflake** — enormous relative to its small frame, pristine and many-pointed, glimmering faintly with something that was not quite ordinary light. With great ceremony it pressed the charm into his hand, retrieved two more from behind the rock, and set them carefully where [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) and [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) would find them in the morning.

It looked back at Aronious once — a look that seemed to say that was entirely the plan all along — and disappeared.

All three party members carry one. On their sheets the snowflake is recorded as the **Charm of the Ice Troll**.

### Old Acquaintances

A chwinga lives in [Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md)'s cave out on the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md), its face marked with a pattern the party found immediately and inexplicably familiar. The verbeeg extended her little finger as she passed, and the creature reached up and touched it gently — a greeting, old and certain, between two beings who had known each other for some time.
