# Shield Guardian

_Construct, Netherese — party companion_

A ten-foot golem of ancient stone and iron, bound to an **amulet** that [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s simulacrum handed over as payment for being made corporeal. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) carries the amulet and commands the construct. It has 64 hit points.

## Recovery

The amulet pulsed with direction — a steady tug toward the mountains like a compass needle finding north — and led the party into a ravine in the [Spine of the World](../atlas/Spine%20of%20the%20World.md) where goblins had built a settlement. The Guardian hung at the centre of the stronghold from rope and timber like a puppet with its strings cut: massive, still, and waiting.

Activating it set off the alarm that burned the stronghold down.

## In Battle

It reads a battlefield with the cold clarity of something that does not feel fear or exhaustion.

At the goblin stronghold it stepped between Aronious and a volley without hesitation, absorbing a dozen arrows as though they were rain, then turned its back to the watchmen and spread itself wide as a ten-foot bulwark with [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) sheltered in its shadow. When the worgs surged at [Polard](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md), a gauntleted hand swept two of them off the ground entirely.

Since then:

- Punched straight through the rotten hull of a shipwreck on the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md) and hit a frost troll hard enough to send it into the far wall
- Plugged the narrow entrance hall of [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md) as a wall of iron against nine enlarged Duergar
- Hammered the [Chardalyn Dragon](Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) in the forge hard enough that light was still leaking from its plates eight hours later — then worked the same flank open at Easthaven with both fists, and got dragged about by the tail for its trouble
- Came through a wall of the crashed nautiloid and destroyed a flesh golem's midsection in one blow

## Stored Smites

Aronious can store a **divine smite** in the Guardian and release it later, and has twice done so without mentioning it to anyone — once before the nautiloid, discharged into the flesh golem, and once more at second level before leaving for [Targos](../atlas/Targos.md).

## Appearance

It came out of the goblin stronghold covered head to toe in goblin graffiti. [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) refused to leave it that way.

The market in [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md) sold them a bottle of _Grizwald's Famous Degreaser_ — tagline _It'll remove anything and everything_ — which the clerk cheerfully admitted was terrible at removing grease but cut through paint as though it had never existed. Too harsh. They put it back.

They bought green, pink, purple, and black paint and a set of brushes instead, and set about making the construct look less like a siege weapon and more like something a person might not immediately flee from. Hobbins applied the finishing touch herself: **a pink peace symbol, carefully painted across the Guardian's forehead.**

It helped. Somewhat.
