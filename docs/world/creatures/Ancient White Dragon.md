# The Ancient White Dragon

_Dragon_

An ancient white dragon that roams [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md) and keeps at least one lair out on the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md). Still alive. Still angry.

## First Sighting

The party saw it before they knew what they were looking at. In a ravine northeast of [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md) they found the back half of a polar bear taken in a single bite, and then a snowdrift that shifted and fell away in a small avalanche to reveal a massive head and neck. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) spotted a **saddle** half-buried in the snow with a robed figure strapped into it, head slumped, arms hanging limp.

They crept past as quietly as they could. On the return journey it had been re-buried by fresh drifts.

## The Invitation

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) reached it by _sending_ on a theory of [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md)'s — that white dragons are benevolent creatures who enjoy the company of people.

> _"Don't panic, we are friendly, need help, Ten-Towns in danger, meet at Redwaters, [Lupine](../characters/Lupine.md) can vouch, at sundown in two days. Ciao!"_

The answer came back low and rasping, like stone grinding against stone.

> _"Come to my lair at the Sea of Moving Ice. You'll find a shipwreck I use as one of my homes."_

Yin Shen's theory was wrong.

## The Ambush

The shipwreck was a hull opened not by rot or weather but by something that had simply decided a wall was an inconvenience, the edges of the breach smooth in a way that suggested size and indifference in equal measure. Inside were a frost troll, a dragon's hoard glimmering under the ice, and four kobolds who confirmed they served it. Asked whether the dragon was friendly toward Ten-Towns, two of them had to sit down.

It arrived the way weather arrives — thunderous wingbeats, the crack of ice giving way, a shadow larger than the hull.

> _"Ahh. I smell the tender flesh and hear the panicked heartbeat of the meal that so eagerly delivered itself to me."_

## Blindness

Its eyes are **filmed over, pale and clouded**. It is ancient enough that its sight has largely failed, and it hunts by smell and sound.

That is the only reason the party survived. [Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md) rolled a fog cloud through the hold in a single dense surge and called them out to her, and they ran for her cave while the dragon answered its own anger with a blast of breath aimed at the sky — spears of ice coming down like thrown lances in a tight and terrible rain.

## Outstanding

[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) and [Mylbor Tarrfak](../characters/Mylbor%20Tarrfak.md) have an agreement to stay in contact: she wants word of this dragon, he wants word of anything worth hunting.

It also sits squarely between [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) and the northern sea route. When [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) suggested Revel's End as the nearest port to [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md), the party pointed out there was a white dragon in the way, and went to [Targos](../atlas/Targos.md) for a ship instead.
