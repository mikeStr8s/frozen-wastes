# Knights of the Black Sword

A group that is holed up in [The Caer](../atlas/The%20Caer.md), not permitting any admittance and showing signs of aggression to those who wish to enter. They answered a polite request for audience with three warning arrows, one of which grazed [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md).

They wear none of [Caer-Dineval](../atlas/Caer-Dineval.md)'s heraldry. The bodies of guards who did are frozen into the walls of the cistern beneath the castle, where the party also found five beaten and shivering servants of the Caer imprisoned.

Speaker [Crannoc Siever](../characters/Crannoc%20Siever.md) has not been seen. His office was empty.

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    The party escaped the Caer and has not returned. Nothing is known about what the Knights of the Black Sword want, who leads them, or what became of [Henry Davis](../characters/Henry%20Davis.md), who was clapped in irons as a traitor for letting them in.
