# Clan Sunblight

_Duergar clan_

[Xardorok Sunblight](../characters/Xardorok%20Sunblight.md)'s clan, and the engine of the [chardalyn](../lore/Chardalyn.md) conspiracy.

Xardorok abandoned a failing realm in the Underdark when the surface offered him grander dreams of conquest, and committed the clan's whole effort to a single weapon: the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md), and a route carved in stone that would walk it through all ten towns.

## Operations

The clan ran a distributed theft operation across Ten-Towns before anyone connected the incidents.

- **[Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md)** — goods, goats, and the magic lantern from [The Northern Light](../atlas/The%20Northern%20Light.md), stolen by a thief whose footprints shrank to nothing at the edge of town
- **[Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md)** — the chardalyn crystal torn out of the town hall figurehead; later, a base of operations on the **Easthaven ferry**, named in [Durth](../characters/Durth.md)'s letter
- **[Kelvin's Cairn](../atlas/Kelvin's%20Cairn.md)** — a stronghold carved into the mountain holding a zombie ogre, the stolen goods, and cells where denizens of Ten-Towns had been transformed into fungal monsters
- **[Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md)** — the seat, in the [Spine of the World](../atlas/Spine%20of%20the%20World.md), and the forge

Their signature is **invisibility and enlargement**: the Easthaven thief was invisible until Yin Shen walked into him, and nine Duergar in the fortress entrance hall enlarged on sight to fill the corridor with grey iron bulk.

They also do not survive capture. Of three prisoners taken, one escaped a cell by shrinking under the door, one drove hidden chardalyn shards into his own flesh and had to be put down, and one was killed at Kelvin's Cairn.

## The Split

The clan was never solidly Xardorok's. **[Grandolpha Muzgardt](../characters/Grandolpha%20Muzgardt.md)**, his own paramour, spent considerable time working from inside his command structure to pull Duergar loyalties toward herself, intending to withdraw her people back into the Underdark and revive the realm he had abandoned.

Her escort walked the party through his forge. Her Duergar took up the chant on the dragon's chains when the chanters died.

When the party left, steel was still meeting steel beyond the walls — the sound of a fortress deciding who it belonged to.

Nobody stayed to learn how it came out.
