# The Frozen Three

_Adventuring company_

The fellowship at the centre of this account — bound not by destiny, as Professor Blinkfizzle has it, but by circumstance, courage, and a most inconvenient frost.

## Members

- **[Aronious III](../characters/Aronious.md)** — a ranger who turned his back on the gods to chase redemption in the snow, and who has since found his way back to an oath by a different road
- **[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md)** — a kitsune rogue of celestial origin, fallen, exiled, and lately heard by [Tyr](../deities/Tyr.md) again
- **[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md)** — a ferret wizard of alarming intellect and no patience whatsoever

They came together at [The Northlook Inn](../atlas/The%20Northlook%20Inn.md) in [Bryn Shander](../atlas/Bryn%20Shander.md), took [Hiln Trollbane](../characters/Hiln%20Trollbane.md)'s contract on [Sephek Kaltro](../characters/Sephek%20Kaltro.md), and have not stopped since.

Two of the three were not strangers. Before her exile, [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) was a goddess with a shrine on the outskirts of a city, and [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) was the paladin posted to guard it — the only one who did not let her pranks drive him off. He crossed several nations looking for her after the pantheon cast her down and refused to say why, and tracked her to Ten-Towns on a report of a man zapped by a lightning rune hidden in a handshake.

He found her in the Northlook with a ferret drinking out of a tankard roughly its own size. He hugged her, then hit her on the head.

## Companions

- **[Polard Hinscrop](../characters/Polard%20Hinscrop.md)** — gnome instrument merchant, joined at [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md); currently on hiatus from adventuring to rebuild his shop
- **The [Shield Guardian](../creatures/Shield%20Guardian.md)** — a Netherese construct bound to Aronious's amulet, painted green, pink, purple, and black with a pink peace symbol on its forehead
- **[Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)** — travelled with them as "Gustavo" until he fled a teleportation circle in the snow; now an employer
- **[Mylbor Tarrfak](../characters/Mylbor%20Tarrfak.md)** — dwarf trophy hunter, answers a _sending_ immediately and enthusiastically

## Standing

The party holds written authority in two towns: a writ from [Torvus](../characters/Torvus.md) deputizing them as **agents of [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md)**, endorsed by [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](../characters/Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) to add that they work **under official business of Easthaven**. See [Ten-Towns](../lore/Ten-Towns.md).

They are also known, since the night of the Chardalyn Dragon, as the ones who killed it — which has proven more useful in a Targos tavern than any amount of sealed wax.
