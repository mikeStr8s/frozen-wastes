# Torg's Trading Company

An independent caravan company that is widely suspected of suspicious shipments. Though most in Ten Town's keep that to themselves since anyone living in the frozen north will do what it takes to survive.

## Party Interactions

The party first came looking for the company at [Trail's End Tavern](../atlas/Trail's%20End%20Tavern.md), where the caravan had taken ten rooms — one of them with its window unlatched and fresh footprints in the snow beneath it. A charmed merchant confirmed that [Sephek Kaltro](../characters/Sephek%20Kaltro.md) was among them, cold in manner and blue in the eye.

By the time the party returned from killing him, the company was gone. Sleds, dogs, and all, departed while they were out in the forest.

## Travel Vouchers

Torg's issues **travel vouchers** redeemable against caravan passage across Ten-Towns. [Torvus](../characters/Torvus.md) of [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md) produced one from his pockets for the party, valid for Eastway travel but not servicing Lonelywood or Dougan's Hole. It took some trouble at the caravan office to prove ownership of it, but it has carried them free of charge ever since.

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) also carries **Torg's journal**, recovered along the way.
