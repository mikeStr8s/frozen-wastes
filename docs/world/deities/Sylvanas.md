# Sylvanas

_God of Nature_

The power the Dale answers to when it answers at all. Sylvanas is not worshipped in Ten-Towns' temples; the god is met in the open, in the cold, by people who already understand the wild.

## Known Servants

**[Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md)**, the verbeeg of the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md), names herself a servant of Sylvanas plainly. She drew her own line on that basis: Ten-Towns' war with the Duergar is not her concern, but the Rime is, because an endless unnatural winter runs against the grain of the world itself.

**[Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md)** came to Sylvanas by a longer road. He had discarded his paladin vows to the pantheon above before he ever reached [Bryn Shander](../atlas/Bryn%20Shander.md), and what found him in the Dale was something older and closer to the ground — a dire wolf godling healed in a cave, a reindeer that did not run when he reached for it, a [Chwinga](../creatures/Chwinga.md) that kept him company through a night watch.

In the forge beneath [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md), with [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) lying singed and still on the stone, he pressed his hand to her chest and called on Sylvanas — the same force behind the reindeer and the Chwinga and the herd in the snow. Healing energy passed through his palm.

She drew a shuddering breath and came back.
