# Auril

_Goddess of Winter Night — the Frostmaiden_

The goddess whose endless Rime holds [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md) in permanent winter. Every town of Ten-Towns makes a **sacrifice to Auril**, and the kind varies: food, warmth, mead — and in [Bryn Shander](../atlas/Bryn%20Shander.md), [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), and [Dougan's Hole](../atlas/Dougan's%20Hole.md), human.

She is not a distant power. The party has seen her.

> Then the temperature dropped sharply. The stars disappeared. The world was sucked into total darkness. Streaking across the sky, barely visible against the black, **Auril** flew — her form casting terrible magics to keep the Rime continuing relentlessly, without mercy or end.

[Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) hurled _magic missile_ at the flying visage out of pure rage. The darts faltered and faded before reaching her. Auril took no notice.

## Her Reach

- **[Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md)** fell to the mortal world marked by Auril's endless Rime.
- **[Sephek Kaltro](../characters/Sephek%20Kaltro.md)**, the blue-eyed killer of Ten-Towns, was said to serve some darker purpose beneath the Frostmaiden's will. He melted into snow when he died.
- **[Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)** was burned at the stake in Easthaven as an offering to her.
- **[Ravison](../characters/Ravison.md)** is a frost druid of Auril who has awakened creatures across Ten-Towns and set them against the people.
- Her magic guards the ice of the **[Reghed Glacier](../atlas/Reghed%20Glacier.md)**, sealing the buried Netherese city of [Ythryn](../atlas/Ythryn.md). Neither fire nor pick will open it.

## Her Island

Out in the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md) lies **[Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md)**, and at its centre the fortress of [Grimskalle](../atlas/Grimskalle.md). The **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)** is kept there — the book [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md) believes holds an incantation that can split the glacier.

The ice mephit **[Soppo](../characters/Soppo.md)** calls it _the lady's book_.

## Opposition

Not everyone in the Dale is resigned to the Rime. The verbeeg [Tinjong](../characters/Tinjong.md), a servant of [Sylvanas](Sylvanas.md), drew a hard line: the fight between Ten-Towns and the Duergar is not her concern, but an endless, unnatural winter pressed against the grain of the world itself is — and anything that moves against Auril can count on her when the time comes.
