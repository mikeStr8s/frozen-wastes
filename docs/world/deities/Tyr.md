# Tyr

_God of Justice_

The god [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) was raised in devotion to, and the god who cast her out.

She was born to a minor god of order and law in Tyr's stern heavenly line. Where the rest of that line carried judgment and discipline, Yin Shen became a patron of tricks, pranks, and joyous laughter — children whispered her name before mischief, trusting that a well-placed jest and a quick hiding place might earn her blessing.

Her fall came through folly rather than malice. Setting out to inconvenience her sister [Shuāng](../characters/Shuāng.md) and the other gods during a festival, she blessed a **child** who asked to cast a higher tier of fire spells — a fire dancer or an entertainer, she assumed, and she did not look further. The festival ended in flames, the offerings destroyed, most of the crowd injured, and some of them dead.

## The Judgement

She stood before the court and pleaded that she had meant no harm. The gods would not listen.

Tyr came down to deliver judgement himself, **without bothering to hear her side**. He stripped her of her title, her [kimono](../artifacts/Yin%20Shen's%20Kimono.md), her magic, her immortality, and her [sword](../artifacts/Black%20Lotus%20Sword.md). He cast her into [Auril](Auril.md)'s realm of frost, and scattered her possessions across [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md).

He did not destroy them. They are still out there.

## The Order That Guarded the Shrines

Tyr's reach extends past his own servants. [Aronious III](../characters/Aronious.md) belonged to an Order sworn to protect the gods themselves, in a city bound to its pantheon by a bargain of offerings and faith in exchange for blessings. He spent years guarding Yin Shen's shrine and became her friend.

When she vanished without a trace and he prayed for an explanation, the pantheon gave him one sentence:

> "because we have declared as such, that is all"

He left the Order that day. The gods' handling of Yin Shen cost them both of their most devoted.

## The Alley in Easthaven

On the night the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) came for [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), Yin Shen found an alley with no one in it and spoke to her sister [Shuāng](../characters/Shuāng.md) for the first time since the fall.

> "I know I fucked up in the past. I don't ask much from you, but please, if you can, help these people out."

Snow began to fall. In a frozen puddle the face looking back at her was golden and pale and not her own, there for a moment and then gone.

Then **the sigil of Tyr melted itself into the ice**.

No weapon came. No spell. But she had been heard, and she walked out of that alley carrying something she had not carried in. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) and [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) each caught a glimpse of a figure who looked a great deal like her — one in a shiny rock at the front of the militia, one as a shape among the stars.

When the dragon was dead, she offered a prayer of thanks to her sister.
