# Tyr

_God of Justice_

The god [Yin Shen](../characters/Yin%20Shen.md) was raised in devotion to, and the god who cast her out.

She was born to a minor god of order and law in Tyr's stern heavenly line. Where the rest of that line carried judgment and discipline, Yin Shen became a patron of tricks, pranks, and joyous laughter — children whispered her name before mischief, trusting that a well-placed jest and a quick hiding place might earn her blessing.

Her fall came through folly rather than malice. A prank aimed at her own sister, another servant of Tyr, ended with a festival in flames, mortals injured, and sacred offerings lost before they could reach the heavens. Her sister carried word of the disaster to Tyr. Judgment was swift, and Yin Shen was cast down to the mortal world before she could plead her case.

## The Alley in Easthaven

On the night the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) came for [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), Yin Shen found an alley with no one in it and spoke to her sister for the first time since the fall.

> "I know I fucked up in the past. I don't ask much from you, but please, if you can, help these people out."

Snow began to fall. In a frozen puddle the face looking back at her was golden and pale and not her own, there for a moment and then gone.

Then **the sigil of Tyr melted itself into the ice**.

No weapon came. No spell. But she had been heard, and she walked out of that alley carrying something she had not carried in. [Aronious](../characters/Aronious.md) and [Hobbins](../characters/Hobbins.md) each caught a glimpse of a figure who looked a great deal like her — one in a shiny rock at the front of the militia, one as a shape among the stars.

When the dragon was dead, she offered a prayer of thanks to her sister.
