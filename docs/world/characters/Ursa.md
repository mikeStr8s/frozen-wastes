# Ursa

_Awakened polar bear, godling_

A godling who has grown discontent with the borders of her realm, and the reason [Lupine](Lupine.md) was lying torn open in a cave when the party found him.

She attacked him seeking to **consume his domain and claim his power for herself**. The fight nearly killed him; his flanks were still oozing into matted grey fur days later, and blue wisps rose off him as he licked at the wounds.

[Aronious](Aronious.md) offered to hunt her. Lupine's eyes flared with something close to panic.

> "No. Do not seek her out alone. She is far beyond you."

He set them on [Skree](Skree.md) instead, and was explicit about the order of things:

> "Skree is a deranged godling. Ursa is merely an ambitious cub. One must fall before the other can be faced."

Skree has fallen. Lupine's wounds have healed and his domain holds for now.

**Ursa remains unfaced.**
