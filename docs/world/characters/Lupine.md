# Lupine

_Awakened Dire wolf, godling_

A powerful and divine creature. Lupine has a tenuous hold on his domain in the eastern wilderness of Icewind Dale. His borderlands are shrinking as a usurper godling, Ursa, has begun to expand their domain.
The party first encountered him in a cave he used for shelter and rest. He was gravely wounded by Ursa in a confrontation in the wilderness. He has asked the party for aid in securing his domain and stopping instability from rising in the region.
