# Lupine

_Awakened Dire Wolf, godling_ — see [Godlings and Divine Energy](../lore/Godlings%20and%20Divine%20Energy.md)

A powerful and divine creature holding a domain in the eastern wilderness of [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md). The party first encountered him in a cave he used for shelter, gravely wounded — his flanks torn open, blue wisps rising from his form — after a confrontation with **Ursa**, a polar bear godling who had grown discontent with the borders of her own realm and attacked him to consume his.

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) approached disguised as a dire wolf pup and tended his wounds with her medicine kit while [Aronious](Aronious.md) spoke the words of _speak with animals_, and understanding bloomed between them.

> "I am Lupine. Once a dire wolf. Now... something more."

He forbade them from hunting Ursa — "She is far beyond you" — and set them a different test: [Skree](Skree.md), a deranged awakened bat whose chaos threatened the balance the godlings keep.

> "Skree is a deranged godling. Ursa is merely an ambitious cub. One must fall before the other can be faced."

## Friendship, Not Company

The party killed Skree and returned with his collar and the corrupt totem they found in his cave. Lupine's wounds had healed. [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) asked him to travel with them, and he refused.

> "I am needed here. My domain requires me. But know this — you have proven yourselves, and you have earned my friendship. Should you face a threat too great to bear alone, call upon me. I will come."

Then he rose to his full height, a blue glow rising off him, and had the party hold their weapons aloft. Frozen air poured from his maw and settled on the steel and wood as delicate ice crystals before fading into them. Both Hobbins and Yin Shen carry the result on their sheets as **Lupine's Boon** — a gift of the dire wolf demigod, +1 to hit and +1 to damage.

His name still carries weight in the Dale. When the party needed an ancient white dragon to take a _sending_ seriously, the message they chose to send was that **Lupine can vouch**.

Ursa remains unfaced.
