# Rinaldo

_Bard, [The White Lady Inn](../atlas/The%20White%20Lady%20Inn.md)_

A self-styled bard of quick fingers and quicker tongue, kept around the White Lady Inn in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) to entertain guests. He is fond of plucking his fiddle while recounting the legend the inn is named for — the White Lady, a ghost said to walk on [Lac Dinneshere](../atlas/Lac%20Dinneshere.md) where her rich husband drowned, a miser who kept his treasure in a heavy locked chest that never left his side.

## The Séance

He announced he would hold a séance to summon her. [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) attended out of curiosity; [Aronious](Aronious.md) stayed upstairs, wary of dealings with spirits.

The candles dimmed and Rinaldo began, voice weaving through false prayers and soft illusions. Then the air shifted — colder, and real. The candles guttered. A thin wisp of silver smoke coiled up from the centre of the room and formed the faint outline of a woman's face.

> "_[Caer-Dineval](../atlas/Caer-Dineval.md)._"

Then she was gone.

Rinaldo went pale and trembling and ushered the guests out at once. Yin Shen lingered, and a single glance from her foxlike eyes drew the truth out of him: his séances had **always been performances**, harmless illusions meant to entertain.

This time, what he summoned answered him.

That one word sent the party to Caer-Dineval, to the [Knights of the Black Sword](../organizations/Knights%20of%20the%20Black%20Sword.md) and the dying prophecy of [Hethyl Arkorran](Hethyl%20Arkorran.md).

The White Lady has not spoken since. Yin Shen searched the lakeshore the following morning and found nothing.
