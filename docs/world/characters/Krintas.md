# Krintas

_Undead attendant_

The undead companion of [Dzaan](../characters/Dzaan.md)'s simulacrum at [The Black Spire](../atlas/The%20Black%20Spire.md). [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) first heard him through a wall, being muttered at over parchment about failed experiments and a missing lifespark.

He accompanied the party and Dzaan down to the chamber of the Netherese machine. When the vortex destabilised, **Krintas shoved the party out of the chamber** — and took the worst of the radiant blast himself, hurled into a wall with his flesh scorched and smouldering.

He left the spire with Dzaan when the simulacrum agreed to travel with the party.
