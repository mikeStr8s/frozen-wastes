# Angajuk

_Awakened whale — legend_

A giant whale with a wrecked ship on its back, awakened once by a **frost druid** to carry travelers across the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md).

There is a **bronze bell on the shore**, the story goes. Ring the one and the other comes.

The party heard it at **Three Flags Sailing** in [Targos](../atlas/Targos.md), from a one-eyed captain who had just finished laughing at the idea of sailing onto the Sea of Moving Ice. Asking for passage, he told [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md), she might as well ring Angajuk's Bell.

He meant it as a dismissal.

[Aronious](Aronious.md) liked the sound of it.

The party chartered a ship instead, and the bell has not been rung.

!!! note
    A frost druid awakening creatures across Icewind Dale is a familiar pattern — see [Ravison](Ravison.md) and [Godlings and Divine Energy](../lore/Godlings%20and%20Divine%20Energy.md). Whether Angajuk is her work or an older story has not been established.
