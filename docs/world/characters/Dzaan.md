# Dzaan

_Human Wizard_

A red-robed wizard of Thay, burned at the stake in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) as an offering to [Auril](../deities/Auril.md). His crime, according to [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md), was murdering the folk of Ten-Towns who had been hired to aid his travels.

That was not the end of him.

## Party Interactions

The party met him at [Trail's End Tavern](../atlas/Trail's%20End%20Tavern.md), where he gave them the [Lantern of Tracking](../artifacts/Lantern%20of%20Tracking.md) and thirty gold for information on [Chwingas](../creatures/Chwinga.md). [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) watched him burn in the town square the following day, and the party inherited his scorched [Gray Bag of Tricks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Bag%20of%20Tricks) and a scroll of [fireball](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Fireball) as a reward from Imdra.

One member of his expedition survived: [Randy Grailden](Randy%20Grailden.md), who sold the party a map to Dzaan's tower.

## The Simulacrum

What the party found at the bottom of the black spire was Dzaan again, shimmering at the edges like heat-haze.

> "Ahh! Visitors! I am Dzaan... or almost."

He was a simulacrum — incomplete, unstable, and cut off from the original wizard's memories from the moment of his creation. He claimed no knowledge of the execution or the crimes attached to his name. In exchange for an amulet and everything he knew about chardalyn, the party worked an ancient Netherese device that made him fully corporeal.

He traveled with them afterward as **"Gustavo"**: shaved clean, hair cropped short, hat confiscated, and his red scarf dyed blue by Yin Shen.

> "Why did it have to be blue..."

He barely slept, reconstructing his spellbook from memory whenever a sled or wagon stopped jolting enough to write. He promised to teach Yin Shen magic.

## Flight and Return

On a cold night camped short of the Sea of Moving Ice, Dzaan lurched upright from his bedroll and carved a teleportation circle freehand into the snow, talking in a tangle — **Red Wizards of Thay**, here in Ten-Towns, looking for him. If they found him alongside the party, the party became a problem they would also need to solve, and he would not allow that. He pressed a handwritten folio of magic into Yin Shen's hands, tried to smile, and vanished in blue-white light.

He returned after the ceremorphs' ship left the valley — blue, translucent, the landscape visible through him. He was astonished to hear the dragon was dead.

Then he told them what he had been doing in Icewind Dale all along: a Netherese city, **Ythryn**, lies buried in the Reghed Glacier. Auril's magic guards the ice, and neither fire nor pick will open it. The **Codicil of White**, on Auril's island out in the Sea of Moving Ice, holds an incantation that will. He wants the party to find it — and to recover **Professor Skant**, his stolen professor orb and an expert on the Netherese Empire. The payoff is the city itself and whatever magic is still lying in it.

!!! note "Canon inconsistency"
    Dzaan names two different pursuers in two different sessions. In Session 20 he is fleeing the **Red Wizards of Thay**; in Session 27 he is dodging the **Arcane Brotherhood**. Both appear in the published story as written and are recorded here unreconciled.
