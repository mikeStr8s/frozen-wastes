# Dzaan

A red mage of Thay. He was put to death by burning at the stake as an offering to Auril. His crimes were that of murdering the folk of Ten Towns according to [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md).

## Party Interactions

The party was approached by him at [Trail's End Tavern](../atlas/Trail's%20End%20Tavern.md). He gave them the [Lantern of Tracking](../artifacts/Lantern%20of%20Tracking.md) and asked for any information they could find on [Chwingas](../creatures/Chwinga.md).
