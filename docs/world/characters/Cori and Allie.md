# Cori and Allie

*Human sisters*

Owners of [The Northern Light](../atlas/The%20Northern%20Light.md) Inn. Kind and caring of their town speaker.

The younger, Allie, is lithe and charming; she greets guests and does all the cleaning. The older, Cori, is stout and scowling; she sees to the inn's provisioning and handles all the cooking.

## The Lantern

The magic lantern that gave the inn its name was stolen, and [Torvus](Torvus.md)' failure to recover it had become a standing joke at his expense. It was Allie who connected the party's account of shrinking footprints in an Easthaven jail cell to the same prints in the snow behind her own storeroom, and who went white when she heard it. She wrote out her account and gave her name as testimony.

Cori, meanwhile, was pouring [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) a glass of something clear off the top shelf before [Aronious](Aronious.md) could stop her, and then handed him a rag and pointed at the floor without a word.

They offered free room and board while the party investigated. They had no coin for a reward, and said so plainly.

When the lantern came back, they rummaged through their belongings and produced a purse holding three gold and six silver — all of it.

> "We're sorry we don't have more to offer you. Times is rough with the winter's Rime shuttering travel, trade, and the joy in our hearts. This is all we have left." — Cori

> "We can offer you rooms and food free of charge whenever you visit!" — Allie

That offer still stands.
