# Dredavex

_Ceremorph, gnome-sized_

The ceremorph at the helm of the crashed nautiloid, working at a control panel beneath a lattice of glowing jellyfish. [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md)'s owl familiar found somewhere to hide on the upper deck and watched for some time without being noticed.

When the trade for the **psi crystal** was agreed, it was Dredavex who brought out a small chitinous chest. Inside were a pair of crystal lenses and a helmet shaped like an owl, capped with something very like a brain.

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) tried the lenses first. They were too big for her and far from comfortable, but she could see in the dark and pick out more of what she was looking at. The helmet wanted attunement, so she took both off again. They proved to be **Eyes of Minute Seeing** and a **Helm of Telepathy**.

Dredavex left with [Voren](Voren.md) and the ship, bound for the Astral realm.
