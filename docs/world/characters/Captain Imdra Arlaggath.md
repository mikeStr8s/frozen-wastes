# Captain Imdra Arlaggath

_Captain of the Easthaven Militia_

A stern, clear-eyed commander who moves faster than the people around her and knows it. She has been the party's most consistent institutional ally in Ten-Towns, and the one who paid for that alliance most dearly.

## Party Interactions

She first met [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) in the square as [Dzaan](Dzaan.md) burned, and on learning that [Sephek Kaltro](Sephek%20Kaltro.md) was dead she set them on the missing fishermen of [Lac Dinneshere](../atlas/Lac%20Dinneshere.md), paying out Dzaan's confiscated effects as the reward.

When the party first brought her the prophecy of a crystal machine with a dragon's heart, she could not believe it, and would not carry wild claims to the Speaker without proof. The writ from [Torvus](Torvus.md) of [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md) changed that. She read it, wrote that the party was **working under official business of Captain Imdra and Easthaven**, and pressed her signet into the wax. She promised notices to every town speaker and warned that it would take time — not everyone moved as quickly as she did.

She was right about the threat and wrong about having it contained. The captured Duergar had hidden chardalyn shards on his person and drove them into his own flesh in confinement; the guards put down what was left of him. She accepted responsibility for the failed interrogation and asked the party's forgiveness. By then three more towns were reinforcing their militias, and half of Ten-Towns stood in some state of readiness.

## Capture and Return

Easthaven was hit hard, fast, and with purpose, and Imdra was taken. The party learned of it from [Oarus Masthew](Oarus%20Masthew.md) in [Termalaine](../atlas/Termalaine.md), along with the location of the stronghold in the Spine of the World that a captured Duergar had given up.

They found her in the cells beneath Xardorok Sunblight's fortress, opened the lock, and put a weapon in her hand before anyone said much else. She looked at them with the expression of someone filing away questions for later, and nodded that she was ready.

She rode back with them for the eight-hour run to Easthaven and left to join the commanders at the wall, bent over the maps while the Chardalyn Dragon came in. Afterward [Aronious](Aronious.md) warned her what prolonged contact with chardalyn does to a mind — a kind of necrosis — and that the fragments in the crater should be buried rather than handled. She put a task force on the cleanup and militia on the site.

She is back on duty in a plain standard uniform. Her own gear never came back out of the fortress.
