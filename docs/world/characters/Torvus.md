# Torvus

*Silver Dragonborn, Town Speaker*

The Town Speaker for [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md), and — by his own appointment — its lead investigator. A known drunk who does not mind being a silver dragonborn half-frozen in a snowdrift, and who is regularly brought in from "patrol" by [Cori and Allie](Cori%20and%20Allie.md) to be fed something warm.

He introduced himself to the party by erupting out of a snowbank and swinging drunkenly for [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md)'s wrist.

> "Who goes there? Is it thieves? Have I found those creeping bastards?"

He had been hunting the town's petty thieves for some time without result, most recently the magic lantern stolen from [The Northern Light](../atlas/The%20Northern%20Light.md), and regaled the party with tales of his adventuring days and how his experience made him perfectly suited to the role.

## The Writ

When the party returned the lantern along with a bound Duergar prisoner and the story of a frozen stronghold, Torvus was genuinely shaken.

> "And here I thought I was looking for petty thieves."

Asked whether he would speak to the other town speakers, he did better. Despite the drink his hand was steady and his words clear. He drafted a legal document **deputizing the party as agents of Caer-Konig**, reporting directly to the town Speaker, and rolled his signet ring into the wax seal.

> "This should go a long way in proving you've got our backing."

Then he emptied his pockets onto the counter — four separate half-empty containers of alcohol came out first — and produced a **Torg's Trading Company travel voucher**, valid for Eastway travel but not servicing Lonelywood or Dougan's Hole.

> "It's no silver bullet, but it'll get you across Ten-Towns no problem."

That writ, endorsed afterward by [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md), is the legal basis for everything the party has done in Ten-Towns since.
