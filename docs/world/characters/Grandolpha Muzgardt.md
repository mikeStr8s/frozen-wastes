# Grandolpha Muzgardt

_Duergar matron, paramour of [Xardorok Sunblight](Xardorok%20Sunblight.md)_

Old in the way that certain things are old — not diminished by it, but concentrated. Long black hair shot through with ribbons of white falls past her shoulders, and her fingernails curve like shards of dark iron.

The party found her behind a door in the western hallway of [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md), seated before a hearty buffet of cooked meats, mushrooms, and stranger fare from deeper places that none of them cared to identify. She looked up, assessed them in a single sweep, and gestured to the empty chairs at her table. Her guards received a different signal: weapons away, back to cooking.

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) ate one bite of something in the spirit of diplomacy, excused herself from the room with as much dignity as the situation allowed, and returned somewhat paler.

## Her Position

She introduced herself as Xardorok's paramour without embarrassment, and what followed explained why. She had no allegiance to him worth the name. For some time she had been working from within his command, quietly pulling Duergar loyalties toward herself and away from his ambitions. Her intention, once the ground was ready, was to withdraw entirely — to take her people back into the Underdark and breathe life into the failing realm Xardorok had abandoned when the surface offered him grander dreams.

> She was not an ally by any warm definition of the word. But her interests and theirs pointed, for the moment, in the same direction.

## The Arrangement

She agreed to a shift change. Her men escorted the party down through a service elevator and into the lower stronghold as though they belonged there, navigating around Xardorok's own throne room guards with the practiced ease of people accustomed to working inside a command structure while quietly subverting it. That escort took the party through the main forge and on to the prison cells, where they found [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md).

In the battle, her Duergar repositioned alongside the chanting circle holding the dragon's runic bonds, and took up the chant themselves when Hobbins killed the chanters — fingers finding the links, voices stumbling into a pattern dead men had left unfinished.

When the dragon broke free and flew north, Grandolpha's Duergar were left holding chains that no longer bound anything.

## Afterward

Steel was still meeting steel beyond the walls as the party left — the sound of a fortress deciding who it belonged to, now that Xardorok lay dead beside his lever. Grandolpha offered no comment on it. In the party's presence she did nothing at all.

Nobody stayed to learn how it came out.
