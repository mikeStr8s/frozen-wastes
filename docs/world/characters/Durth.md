# Durth

_Duergar, son of [Xardorok Sunblight](Xardorok%20Sunblight.md)_

Known to the party only through a crumpled letter found in the Duergar stronghold at [Kelvin's Cairn](../atlas/Kelvin's%20Cairn.md), written to the brother who was holding that outpost:

> _Brother,_
> _You will find me on the frozen ferry in Easthaven. From this new base, the search for chardalyn continues. Long may our father reign over this dark land!_
> _— Durth_

That letter is what connected the thefts in [Caer-Konig](../atlas/Caer-Konig.md) and [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) to a single operation, and gave [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) something concrete enough to act on. The **Easthaven ferry** was named as a base of operations.

His brother at the Kelvin's Cairn outpost — the Duergar in finery, pinned in the spike trap — was the one who bellowed the boast about their father's dragon.

Durth's own fate is unrecorded. He was not among the dead at [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md) so far as the party knows.
