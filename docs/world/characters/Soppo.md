# Soppo

_Ice mephit, self-appointed guide to [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md)_

A small ice mephit that was on the shattered giants' dock waving and jabbering as the party's longboat came in, riding low with the [Shield Guardian](../creatures/Shield%20Guardian.md) aboard.

It speaks **Auran**. [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) cast _comprehend languages_ as a ritual and has done the talking ever since, passing along what comes back.

Soppo offered to be their guide. There is plenty to see, by its account: shipwrecks full of goods, a field of ice sculptures, and a recent wreck with one survivor who made it as far as the shore and froze there.

> "Soppo hasn't had visitors in a while. Again, unless you count the one who crawled ashore, though she didn't last."

Asked about [Professor Skant](../artifacts/Professor%20Skant.md), it did not know the orb.

Asked about the **[Codicil of White](../artifacts/The%20Codicil%20of%20White.md)**, it looked shocked, and called it _the lady's book_. That is at [Grimskalle](../atlas/Grimskalle.md), at the centre of the island, where it is dangerous.

The tour it proposed runs fourteen hours with Grimskalle at the end. The party accepted.

[Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) kept her guard up.
