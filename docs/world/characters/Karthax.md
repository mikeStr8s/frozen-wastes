# Karthax

_Dragonborn, Armorer of Termalaine_

The armorer in [Termalaine](../atlas/Termalaine.md). [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) brought him the tentacles cut from the grell killed in the town's mines and laid them on his workbench. He turned them over, considered the tensile quality of the tissue, and told her to return in the morning.

He had the work done by then: the tentacles cured and lashed with leather bindings into a serviceable **Grell Tentacle Whip**. Yin Shen took it without ceremony and has carried it since.
