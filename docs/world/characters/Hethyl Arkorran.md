# Hethyl Arkorran

An ancient and venerable dwarf with a wooden peg where her right foot should be. Blunt, and to the point she knows she doesn't have much time left.
## Party Interactions

Gives her last prophecy to the party before passing due to age and the strain of the foretelling.

> _"Hidden away, buried in the mountains, lies a fortress, a villain, a crystal machine with a dragon's heart! O, mighty wings beating and breath like the sun! Bright enough to BURN away the Rime — to burn us all if you do... not... stop..."_

Every word of it came true. The fortress was [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md), the villain [Xardorok Sunblight](Xardorok%20Sunblight.md), and the crystal machine the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md). She also stressed, before the prophecy took her, that the [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md) were the danger and must be stopped.
