# Hansel Wiresnap

_Gnome, Instrument Merchant_

A small, if enigmatic, gnome merchant located in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md). He owns a shop called Drums Emporium and specializes in the percussion instruments. He is in partnership with his cousin [Finkle Shortfuse](Finkle%20Shortfuse.md) in a small musical enterprise. What one doesn't sell, the other has on hand.
