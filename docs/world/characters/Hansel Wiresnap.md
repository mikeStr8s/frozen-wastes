# Hansel Wiresnap

_Gnome, Instrument Merchant — an alias of [Polard Hinscrop](Polard%20Hinscrop.md)_

A small, if enigmatic, gnome merchant located in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), owner of **Drums Emporium**, specialist in percussion, and cousin and business partner to [Finkle Shortfuse](Finkle%20Shortfuse.md).

Hansel does not exist either. He is [Polard Hinscrop](Polard%20Hinscrop.md) in a blue shirt — the same gnome, the same back room, a different change of clothes and a slightly flustered entrance.

Of the two aliases, Hansel was the one quietly dropped. **Drums Emporium** was found locked and emptied on the party's return to Easthaven: shelves bare, dust in the air, and the broken trapdoor between the shops replaced with seamless new flooring. [Aronious](Aronious.md) kicked the door down out of concern and found nothing inside at all.
