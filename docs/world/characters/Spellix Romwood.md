# Spellix Romwood

_Gnome, Merchant_

A gnome merchant whose caravan was ambushed by goblins in the mountain pass entrance to [Icewind Dale](../atlas/Icewind%20Dale.md), losing his goods and companions in the raid. In a desperate attempt to free his people, he fashioned a goblin disguise — only to accidentally stumble into the role of goblin chief, through a series of events he described as "immediately going wrong in every direction." He remained trapped in the role, certain that goblin elders had seen through his ruse and were plotting against him.

He revealed himself to the party in a room he called the "chatting chamber," by closing the door and pulling his own head off.

Rescued during their raid on the goblin stronghold, he fled the burning fortress alongside them and escaped to [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md).

He thanked them with genuine warmth, explained he would be staying at the inn briefly before heading south to his family, then turned out his pockets to reveal they were entirely empty and shrugged — perhaps the inn was too much to hope for.

[Aronious](Aronious.md) pressed two gold into his hand without a word.
