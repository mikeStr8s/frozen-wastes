# Spellix Romwood

_Gnome, Merchant_

A gnome merchant whose caravan was ambushed by goblins in the mountain pass entrance to Icewind Dale, losing his goods and companions in the raid. In a desperate attempt to free his people, he fashioned a goblin disguise — only to accidentally stumble into the role of goblin chief. He remained trapped in the role, certain that goblin elders had seen through his ruse and were plotting against him.

Rescued by the party during their raid on the goblin stronghold. He fled the burning fortress alongside them and escaped to [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md).
