# Polard Hinscrop

_Gnome, Instrument Merchant and Adventurer (retired)_

The gnome behind both halves of the [Music Shop Conspiracy](../organizations/Music%20Shop%20Conspiracy.md) — proprietor of **Strings and Sundries**, proprietor of **Drums Emporium**, and sole performer of the roles of "Finkle Shortfuse" and "Hansel Wiresnap." He is embarrassed by his real name, which he considers distinctly un-gnomish, and gave it up only under pressure.

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) gave him the business advice that saved him, and he has treated her as a mentor ever since.

## Adventuring Career

After merging his two shops under "new management," Polard took up [Aronious](Aronious.md)'s invitation and joined the party, arriving in a suit of armor he had made himself: a metal pasta strainer for a helmet, bent cymbals for shoulder pads, and a hodgepodge of kitchen utensils, instrument parts, and leather straps for the rest. The dwarven smiths of [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) inspected it, laughed, and then admitted the craftsmanship was genuinely good and that it would stop a blade.

At the goblin stronghold, with arrows falling on his companions, something in him turned over.

> "Not at my friends!"

Every seam of the armor sealed and melted flush, the whole of it fusing into one immaculate piece — and then growing, swelling to ten feet tall as a deep resonant voice rolled out from inside it. A lightning hammer ignited in his fist. He has fought that way since, at the burning stronghold and again in the entrance hall and throne room of Xardorok Sunblight's fortress.

## Retirement

The Chardalyn Dragon's radiant breath went straight down Polard's lane in Easthaven and left nothing standing. He went to his knees in the rubble and wept. When [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) asked whether the shop had been insured, he produced the pamphlet, turned to the last page, and showed them the final unchecked box.

> "I never got it notarized."

A week later the town had doubled his plot in thanks for his part in the defense. He told the party he was stepping back from adventuring — a hiatus, a temporary retirement, as he put it — and meant to install a forge and train with the Easthaven blacksmith so that he could supply the party's gear himself. He cried as they left, and asked them to visit often.

The rebuilt shop is larger than the old one and about half finished. He has hired an assistant named Samantha. At the party's suggestion he renamed it the **Musical Empolardium**, and did not need to think about it:

> "Samantha, we need to change the sign immediately."

Asked again after his brother, he hesitated, stepped into the back room, and came out in a different shirt as Finkle Shortfuse. Aronious caught the flicker of confusion and accepted the brother anyway.
