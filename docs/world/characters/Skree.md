# Skree

_Awakened Giant Bat, godling (deceased)_

A corrupted divine creature who dwelt in a small cave complex in the mountains southeast of [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md). [Lupine](Lupine.md) named him a danger to all who wandered near and set him as the party's proof of worth.

He was a horror to look at by the end. His once-sleek fur was matted and torn, his body riddled with pustules and rot, wounds weeping across his wings and flanks — and a wrongness radiated off him that went deeper than madness.

## Death

The party reached him through a spider den, past hundreds of bats spiralling through the dark. [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) had chosen her spells for the fight, hurling thunderous orbs that struck at his keen and fragile senses, while [Aronious](Aronious.md)'s dire wolf tore bats from the air and [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) thinned the swarm from the shadows.

A sonic pulse from Skree shook the cave and dropped Aronious where he stood. Hobbins and Yin Shen doubled their assault, and the godling fell from the air to the stone below.

Hobbins recovered [Skree's Collar](../artifacts/Skree's%20Collar.md) and his charm from the body as proof for Lupine.

## The Corruption

The wrongness did not die with him. The party followed it to a shadowed alcove and found a dark totem radiating the same foul energy that had poisoned him — see the [Off-putting Effigy](../artifacts/Off-putting%20Effigy.md). See also [Godlings and Divine Energy](../lore/Godlings%20and%20Divine%20Energy.md). Hobbins wrapped it in cloth to dull its influence and carried it out.

Whatever corrupted Skree was not Skree's own doing, and its source has not been found.
