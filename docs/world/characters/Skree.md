# Skree

_Awakened Giant Bat, godling_

A corrupted and divine creature. Skree is a Giant Bat who dwells in a small cave complex in the mountains to the southeast of [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md). Skree is the first target of the godling [Lupine](Lupine.md) in an attempt to bolster his domain while removing a dangerous foe.
