# Randy Grailden

*Human*

The sole surviving member of the expedition [Dzaan](Dzaan.md) took into the wilderness. He made it back alive and alone, and was recovering at [The White Lady Inn](../atlas/The%20White%20Lady%20Inn.md) in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) when [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) found him.

He clammed up the moment she asked, hands shaking, and kept eating.

> "I don't know nothing. He took me, is all."

She pressed him until he understood that she knew he had been to the tower, knew he had come back, and knew everyone had a price. He worked his own out loud.

> "Fifteen gold to get out of this hell hole, and five more to keep me comfy for a short while... yes, yes, that should do."

He asked for twenty. She paid without hesitation, and he sketched the route to Dzaan's tower onto her map with a trembling hand.

That map led the party to the black spire — and to what was waiting at the bottom of it.
