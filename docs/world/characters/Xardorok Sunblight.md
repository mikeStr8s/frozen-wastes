# Xardorok Sunblight

_Duergar, Lord of Clan Sunblight (deceased)_

The Duergar lord whose ambition drove the whole of the chardalyn conspiracy. He abandoned a failing realm in the Underdark for grander dreams of conquest on the surface, and spent them building one weapon.

He was first known to the party only as a boast. A Duergar in finery, pinned in a spike trap beneath [Kelvin's Cairn](../atlas/Kelvin's%20Cairn.md), bellowed in Dwarvish:

> My father is more powerful than any of you filthy creatures could imagine! You don't understand the glory of it, the power found in the ice. None of you do. But you will learn soon enough when my father unleashes his terror upon Ten-Towns! Your doom soars on dragon's wings!

The soothsayer [Hethyl Arkorran](Hethyl%20Arkorran.md) had already seen it, years out and wrapped in prophecy: _a fortress, a villain, a crystal machine with a dragon's heart._

## The Forge

The party found him at last in [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md), on a raised platform above his main forge — a pyramid of stone ten feet tall with its apex sheared off, pulsing slow and deep from within like something breathing. Chained beneath an opening in the ceiling, runic links burning against dark crystal hide, was the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md), vast and black and furious in the particular way of something powerful that has been made to hold still.

He watched his work with the satisfaction of a man who has built something terrible and knows it.

A stone relief table in the upper fortress showed what he intended: Ten-Towns carved in miniature, a channel cut between all ten settlements in a continuous loop, and a dragon figurine on an iron pole that a crank walked around the circuit. Someone had been plotting the route for a long time.

## Death

His own paramour betrayed him. [Grandolpha Muzgardt](Grandolpha%20Muzgardt.md) had been quietly pulling Duergar loyalties away from him for some time, and it was her escort that walked the party through his forge unchallenged.

When the fight came he fought with a **black crystal gauntlet** that tore the floor open with hellfire from a plane that had no business being involved. He put [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) through the floor with it and dropped [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) singed and still to the stone — she came back only because [Aronious](Aronious.md) ran to her and called on Sylvanas.

He went invisible. It bought him nothing: Yin Shen had him bound with _witch bolt_ and Aronious had laid _hunter's mark_ on the same thread. He emerged on the far side of the forge and threw a lever set into the wall, bringing ice cascading through the ceiling.

He was still pulling it when the party descended on him.

> He fell beside his lever, alone in the way that men who trade loyalty for power tend to find themselves at the end.

His death did not stop the dragon. The chains went slack, the runes failed, and it launched itself up through the hole in the ceiling and north over Icewind Dale.
