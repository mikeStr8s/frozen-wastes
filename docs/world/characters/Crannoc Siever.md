# Crannoc Siever

_Human, Town Speaker of Caer-Dineval_

The Speaker for [Caer-Dineval](../atlas/Caer-Dineval.md). According to **Roark** of [The Uphill Climb](../atlas/The%20Uphill%20Climb.md), he keeps to [The Caer](../atlas/The%20Caer.md) these days and is not seen in the town.

According to the guards on the wall of the Caer, he is unwell and sees no one.

!!! warning "Unverified"
    No one in the party has laid eyes on Crannoc Siever. The claim that he is sick and shut up in the Caer comes from the [Knights of the Black Sword](../organizations/Knights%20of%20the%20Black%20Sword.md), who hold the castle, answered a polite request for audience with three warning arrows, and were found to be wearing none of Caer-Dineval's heraldry — while the bodies of guards who were are frozen into the walls of the cistern below.

    His office was empty when the party reached it. Only tax documents and scattered parchment were on the desk.
