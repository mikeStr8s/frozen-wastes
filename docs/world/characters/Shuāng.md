# Shuāng

_Celestial, servant of [Tyr](../deities/Tyr.md) — twin sister of [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md)_

The other half of the pair. Where [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) went into the shadows, Shuāng stood in the open in heavy, deliberately loud armor and drew every eye in the room.

She is a devoted follower of Tyr and defended the celestial realm against the evil spirits that threatened it, which she did gladly. Her weapon is **the Dragon's Glaive**, its blade coated in a purifying flame no evil spirit survives — and which doubles, usefully, as a lie detector.

Side by side the twins were close to unbeatable. Shuāng took the attention and the front of the fight; Yin Shen came in silent from behind with the [black lotus sword](../artifacts/Black%20Lotus%20Sword.md).

## Temperament

Shuāng was the one who carried out Yin Shen's punishments — the language drills, the fragile objects to be cleaned, the inventories to be catalogued, the letters of apology, the full days spent stuck in fox form.

She found her sister exhausting. She also privately admired how creative the pranks could get, and would never have said so out loud. At the end of it they were family, and they cared for each other.

Her own time went to writing poetry, practising her techniques and her flute, trying new kinds of tea, meditating, and — occasionally — eating sweets.

## The Exile

When the festival ended in fire and bodies, **it was Shuāng who carried word of it to Tyr.**

She is a servant of justice and she behaved like one. The party's record names her plainly as *"the one who had brought about her exile."*

At the trial Yin Shen could bear the gods refusing to listen. What she could not bear was her own sister — the one who had put her in front of that court — unable to look in her direction. She did not want her family believing she had some twisted dark side to her.

The last thing she saw as Tyr cast her down was her family crying. She does not know whether they wept because she was banished or because they thought she was a monster.

Whether Shuāng expected Tyr to go as far as he did is not recorded. She did not speak for her.

## Since

Yin Shen prayed to her in an alley in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) on the night the [Chardalyn Dragon](../creatures/Chardalyn%20Dragon.md) came, for the first time since the fall.

Snow began to fall. A golden, pale face that was not Yin Shen's own looked back out of a frozen puddle, and the sigil of Tyr melted itself into the ice. No weapon came and no spell, but she had been heard.

Yin Shen offered a prayer of thanks when the dragon was dead.
