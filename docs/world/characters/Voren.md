# Voren

_Ceremorph, gnome-sized_

One of the ceremorphs aboard the crashed nautiloid in the far north, and the one who did the talking. [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) heard Gnomish through a door on the middle deck, knocked, and went in alone.

Voren was repairing the ship. He explained it had been a research mission: they collected creatures, two **carrion crawlers** and a larger beast that tunnels. After the crash there was no way to feed them, so they let all three go — which accounts for the things that came up out of the snow and dragged [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) toward the hull.

They answer, he said, to something called **the mother brain**. He insisted they were not controlled, not the way other mind flayers are.

Then his scanner found a power source on the other side of the wall, which was where Yin Shen was standing.

> "The visitors on the ship, yes, they might be able to help. I sure hope our golem hasn't done any damage to them."

Asked whether he would come to the party's aid if called by _sending_:

> "No, I don't think we can do that. We gotta get out of here. We've already caused too much trouble being stuck in this area. If someone discovers us, they'll definitely take us down."

## The Trade

When Yin Shen came in with the **psi crystal** she had taken from a mind flayer's skull in the Termalaine mines, Voren stopped using his mouth and spoke inside their heads instead. He named the crystal, and showed them the one burnt out in the ship's propulsion.

He refused to trade future transport. An item he would do. [Dredavex](Dredavex.md) produced a small chitinous chest holding a pair of crystal lenses and an owl-shaped helmet capped with something very like a brain — later identified as **Eyes of Minute Seeing** and a **Helm of Telepathy**.

The ceremorphs were not upset about the golem the party had destroyed. They would rather get off the planet than dwell on it.

## Departure

Voren was plain about the rest. They were bound for the **Astral realm**. The party could not come, and after that he did not expect anyone would be able to reach them.

The tentacles tore free, the ship rose, and then it was a streak in the sky, and then it wasn't.
