# Terence Brightbrow

_Gnome Wizard, party boy of the university in Waterdeep_

A gnome in the same robe and the same hat as [Hobbins](Hobbins.md), working a room with finger guns that shoot confetti, balloons, and mage hands. He hosts a speakeasy beneath a disused dog sled kennel in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md) — an empty shack fifteen feet across with a hatch in the floor, and beneath the hatch a metal spiral staircase winding down into a cavern that has no business fitting under it, packed with partygoers from more regions than Easthaven sees in a year.

Hobbins knew him. He was the party boy of the university in Waterdeep, and he bullied her for being a nerd.

He denied it.

> "Hey, don't try and harsh my mellow. I'm here for a party for everybody."

Then his finger slipped, and a streamer unfurled across the room reading **_no nerds allowed_**.

He bought the room a round, and every eye in the cavern began to glow blue. Hobbins left on friendly terms with a bottle of the same white liquor under her arm, telling him that if he wanted to find her he could ask the townsfolk.

## What He Is Actually Capable Of

Six Easthaven guards raided the shack above over unpaid taxes, carrying cudgels only. Hobbins hid in a hay-filled box and watched through the slats: the out-of-town guests left by portal, and the guards came back up with nothing but Easthaven locals and no explanation for why the place had been bigger a moment before.

When they were gone she went back down. The staircase ended in the kennel's cold-storage cellar — frozen, small, and entirely ordinary.

Hobbins put it at something like _mighty fortress_. Eighth-level conjuration.

From a man whose reputation does not reach anywhere near it.
