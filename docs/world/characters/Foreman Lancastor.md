# Foreman Lancastor

_Human, Miners Guild Foreman of Termalaine_

Broad shouldered man with a gravely voice and a no nonsense attitude. Leader of the local miners guild and cares deeply for the men he commands.

## The Lost Miners

The party found him behind his desk in the mining offices after reading the notice on the [Gelp](../organizations/Gelp.md) board: _Miners Lost! 50g Reward._ The guild had refused all work until the men were found and the mine declared safe.

He laid it out without ceremony. Miners were missing, kobolds had moved in, and no one on his crew was going back down until something was done about it.

What came back out of the mine was not what he expected. [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) had freed the kobolds from the ghost that had been steering them into banditry and offered them a deal — work the mines for [Termalaine](../atlas/Termalaine.md) free of charge, in exchange for meals and lodging in town. Lancastor looked at the three of them for a long time, then said he needed to check with the Speaker first and left the room with the expression of a man buying himself time to think.

He came back with [Oarus Masthew](Oarus%20Masthew.md), who took the deal.
