# Finkle Shortfuse

_Gnome, Instrument Merchant — an alias of [Polard Hinscrop](Polard%20Hinscrop.md)_

A small, if enigmatic, gnome merchant located in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), owner of **Strings and Sundries**, specialist in wood and string instruments, and for a long while the senior partner in a small musical enterprise with his cousin [Hansel Wiresnap](Hansel%20Wiresnap.md). What one didn't sell, the other had on hand.

Finkle does not exist. He is [Polard Hinscrop](Polard%20Hinscrop.md) in a red shirt.

The party unravelled it by carrying him bodily from one shop to the other while he was still insisting he was two people. Cornered, he confessed the whole arrangement.

Finkle was retired shortly afterward — "sold" the shop to his brother and left town for warmer lands in the south, which is to say he stopped wearing the red shirt. [Aronious](Aronious.md) was genuinely saddened by the farewell.

He came out of retirement once, in the back room of the Musical Empolardium, when the party asked Polard after his brother one time too many.
