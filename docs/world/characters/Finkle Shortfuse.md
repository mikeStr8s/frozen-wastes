# Finkle Shortfuse

_Gnome, Instrument Merchant_

A small, if enigmatic, gnome merchant located in [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md). He owns a shop called Strings and Sundries and specializes in the sale of wood and string instruments. He is in partnership with his cousin [Hansel Wiresnap](Hansel%20Wiresnap.md) in a small musical enterprise. What one doesn't sell, the other has on hand.
