# Ravison

*Frost Druid of Auril*

An elusive frost druid. A frost druid of [Auril](../deities/Auril.md). She has awakened creatures around Ten-Towns like the Plesiosaur found in [Maer Dualdon](../atlas/Maer%20Dualdon.md), which she commanded to make the people of [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md) suffer. See [Godlings and Divine Energy](../lore/Godlings%20and%20Divine%20Energy.md).
