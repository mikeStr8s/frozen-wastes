# Aronious III — Player Backstory


!!! note "Player-written"
    This is Aronious's player's own text, published as written. The condensed in-world version lives at [Aronious III](../Aronious.md).

---

Aronious, a paladin of the blessed city, belonged to an Order tasked with the divine duty of protecting the Gods. This was due to the bond between a pantheon of multiple gods, who helped the city through blessings and miracles, in exchange for offerings, faith and protection.

As a child, Aronious had grown up watching miracles and divine interventions happen time and time again, and could not help but be amazed. The feeling of hope and inspiration that brewed inside him over time turned into solid determination. Determination to protect exactly what has inspired him as a child.

After a few years in the order, Aronious became both strong and respected enough to receive a divine task, to protect one of the holy shrines.
As a part of the deal with the gods, the city will receive help from the gods in exchange for offerings and worship, which happened at these shrines. Without the shrines, that part of the deal would be broken, and the gods mercy could turn sour. Therefore, dependable paladins were placed at these shrines to ensure the bond between gods and the city stayed true and pure. Thus making the task of protecting these shrines one of the greatest honors a paladin of the order could be given.

The shrine Aronious was dispatched to, was somewhat unexpected... In the outskirts of the town, far away from the dense population, was a shrine not too particularly big, and with few offerings to speak of. The little offerings there were to see, looked like children's toys and scrap, such as whoopie cushions, horns, heck even a small rune to give a minor shock during a handshake. All things that are... well, mischievous. That's when he spotted something peeking from behind the shrine. A small creature, resembling a fox, sneering at him before disappearing into the air.

Across the following years, Aronious learned the true reason why this post was empty before he took over. Every day was filled with pranks and tricks by the holy being of the shrine, which had driven away the past Paladins stationed there.. Although he had never spoken to the deity of the shrine, it was easy to summarize which deity it belonged to. This was clearly the work of the goddess of mischief, Yin Shen, one of the less popular deities in the pantheon, but nevertheless one to respect.

Throughout the remainder of his service, Aronious and Yin Shen grew close, with Aronious making sure to keep her pranks to a manageable level, and making sure she didn't get out of line.

Atleast... that's how it used to be, before she was cast out from the pantheon. It all happened so suddenly, one day he was cleaning up after a silly pie-trap that made a little mess, and the next she was just.. gone, without a trace.
Desperate, Aronious prayed to the pantheon for answers, for an explenation, for anything. He was met with but one sentence in the language of the divine, spoken directly into his mind... "because we have declared as such, that is all"

Aronious fell to the floor... this?... THIS is the words of the gods he had commited himself to for so long? THIS is the divine beings he was inspired by as a child? No, it couldn't be. These.. things, were not what he had worshipped for so long. Just the image he had believed them to be... but no longer. No longer would he commit his life to protect these unjust gods, whom would exile a fellow god without as much as a trace of reason. The goddess he had spent the last few years protecting and befriending no less. He would not allow this.

Without hesitation, Aronious left the order, his city, and his now meaningless duty behind. Only two things were clear in his mind. He would find Yin Shen, and bring her back. While also finding himself... and his purpose.

Along the way, Aronious discovered a lot... the art of the bow, the druid's and their connection to nature, and uncanny instincts to survive in the wild.

After traveling across multiple nations, Aronious found a trail leading towards a land of cold and blizzards. A place where winter had consumed the once green and bright grass that stretched as far as the eye could see. A place called Ten Towns.

He had gotten solid tips from a civilian who found themselves zapped during a handshake by a small lightning rune in the hand... classic Yin Shen, it had to be her.
Although unused to the harsh winter terrain, he carried on, until he reached the first town he could spot. As he burst through a door to the local tavern, cold and out of breath, he looked around. Some dwarves... some humans... something he could only assume is three goblins in a trench coat. And last but not least, a... a ferret? Drunk inside a mug of beer, clearly as big as the ferret itself, talking to someone.. but that voice talking to the ferret, it was familiar. It was Yin Shen! He rushed over to hug her, before promptly hitting her head, demanding to know what the hell she was doing in a tavern with a drunk-beer-bathing ferret.

After briefly catching up, warming up, and.. sobering up for the ferret atleast:
The story begins
