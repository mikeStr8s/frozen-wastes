# Hobbins — Player Backstory


!!! note "Player-written"
    This is Hobbins's player's own text, published as written. The condensed in-world version lives at [Hobbins](../Hobbins.md).

---

1. Origin

Hobbins was born in Good Mead to the well-known Hobbins family, owners of The Magikal Morgue, a successful magical curiosity shop. Despite the name, the business was perfectly legitimate—if a little unsettling to first-time visitors. The Hobbins family specialized in buying magical items recovered from the frozen wastes, whether salvaged from the remains of unlucky adventurers or sold by the rare survivors who made it back alive. Every item was carefully cataloged, restored when possible, and sold to collectors, travelers, and aspiring adventurers.

Her mother, Logely Hobbins, handled the books and negotiations with sharp precision, while her father, Drumly Hobbins, had an uncanny eye for authentic magical relics. Together they built a small fortune, making the Hobbins family one of the more prosperous households in Good Mead.

Hobbins grew up surrounded by enchanted trinkets, cursed curiosities, and stories of impossible adventures. She loved all of it, but she also loved the part her parents never quite understood: the thrill of doing the right thing in the most inconvenient, ridiculous way possible. If someone needed help, Hobbins was the sort to sneak out after curfew, steal back a stolen relic, and leave behind a glittering illusion of a goose wearing a crown just to confuse the culprit.

Her parents believed adventuring itself was a terrible investment. In their eyes, adventurers were simply future customers—or worse, future inventory. They had spent years watching ambitious heroes throw away promising futures chasing treasure, only to return broke... or not return at all. Magic was valuable. Knowledge was valuable. But risking your life for either was foolish when you could build a comfortable life studying and trading them instead.

Recognizing Hobbins' talent for the arcane, they sent her to study at the prestigious magical academies of Waterdeep, believing she would become a respected scholar, enchanter, or merchant capable of expanding the family business far beyond Icewind Dale.

2. The Turn

Life in Waterdeep changed Hobbins in ways her parents never expected.

For the first time, she was surrounded by ambitious wizards, dangerous experiments, and libraries filled with knowledge no one in Good Mead had ever dreamed of. While she excelled in her studies, she also developed a reputation for harmless—but relentless—mischief. Illusions, disappearing books, enchanted ink, and practical jokes became almost as common as her spellcasting. If a professor was being cruel, Hobbins was likely to "accidentally" ruin their lecture notes. If a classmate was being bullied, she was just as likely to slip them a helpful charm and a perfectly timed distraction.

During school breaks, Hobbins became close friends with a dwarf janitor at the academy, and the two spent their time teaching each other things between chores and quiet moments in the halls. From him, Hobbins learned dwarvish, picking up the language little by little whenever school was out on break.

The more she learned, the more the quiet life her parents envisioned felt like a cage. Every ancient spell referenced forgotten ruins. Every professor spoke of discoveries waiting beyond civilization. Adventure stopped sounding reckless—it sounded necessary. Not because she wanted glory, but because there were too many people out there who needed help, too many secrets buried under snow, and far too many rules standing in the way of doing what was right.

When Hobbins announced she intended to leave Waterdeep and become an adventurer, her parents strongly opposed the idea. They argued that every treasure worth finding eventually found its way into a shop like theirs without risking one's own neck. Why become the frozen corpse when you could buy from the people who survived?

Hobbins disagreed.

3. The Wound

Hobbins left home on good terms, but not happy ones.

Her parents never truly approved of her decision, believing she was throwing away everything they had worked to provide. Every letter from home politely encouraged her to "come to her senses" and return to the family business before Icewind Dale claimed another foolish explorer.

Deep down, Hobbins wants to prove that adventure is more than greed or recklessness. She wants to show her family that the knowledge, friendships, and discoveries made beyond the safety of a shop can be just as valuable as anything sitting on a shelf. More than that, she wants to prove that a person can be kind, useful, and wildly inconvenient all at once—that breaking a few rules to help someone is not a flaw, but a feature.

Though she would never admit it aloud, every successful expedition feels like another argument in a conversation she's still trying to win.

4. The Drive

Hobbins seeks forgotten magic, ancient relics, and knowledge lost beneath the ice—not simply to become powerful, but to prove that true discovery can only come from those willing to leave the safety of home.

One day she hopes to return to The Magikal Morgue carrying artifacts no merchant could have purchased because no one else had ever found them.

She also refuses to let the harshness of Icewind Dale change who she is. Even in dangerous places, she delights in confusing enemies, teasing allies, and finding absurdly clever ways to help people. If a plan is too orderly, she will probably improve it. If a problem can be solved with a little chaos and a lot of heart, Hobbins is already halfway there.

5. The Tie

Logely Hobbins – Mother. Brilliant businesswoman and co-owner of The Magikal Morgue. Loves Hobbins deeply but believes adventuring is an unnecessary gamble.
Drumly Hobbins – Father. Expert appraiser of magical relics. Thinks Hobbins is talented enough to become one of the greatest magical merchants in the North—if she survives long enough to realize it.
The Magikal Morgue – The Hobbins family's magical shop in Good Mead, known for buying and selling magical items recovered from Icewind Dale's frozen wilderness.
The magical academies of Waterdeep – Where Hobbins spent much of her life studying wizardry and becoming notorious for her magical pranks.
