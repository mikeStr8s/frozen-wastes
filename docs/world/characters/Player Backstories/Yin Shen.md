# Yin Shen — Player Backstory


!!! note "Player-written"
    This is Yin Shen's player's own text, published as written. The condensed in-world version lives at [Yin Shen](../Yin%20Shen.md).

---

The story of yin shen begins in the celestial heavens, realm of the gods. Tho not a god herself she was a lesser god. Goddess of trickery and mischief. The kind of tricks that are (usually) harmless in nature. She was raised under the rule of tyr, god of justice. Her family followed his rule. (Atleast most of her family) While her sister and her mother followed tyr, her father thought it was ok to “bend” the rules. As long as nothing bad happened and no one was hurt, it didnt matter too much. She even became friends with a mortal. A human named aronious III, a paladin, who was all about righteousness and good, and was everything that she despised about Tyr. But he made good company for fooling around with and talking about gossip. She spent most of her afternoons (when not in the heavens) communing with Aron, constantly trying to get him to worship her. And annoying him, talking about how she got away with annoying the gods. She was a prodigy of mischief, knowing exactly what buttons to push without going too far. And in time, she did get worshipped for it. (Not by Aron) but by the fun troublemaking children who were up to no good. And when she got worshipped, she got bolder, more risky with her tricks. She was a constant nuisance to her sister, but as much as her sister was annoyed with yin shen, she still loved yin shen. And loved how creative she could get with her pranks. Because at the end of the day, they were family, and they always cared for each other. Although a trouble maker, she was a force to be reckoned with on the battlefield. Side by side with her twin, Shuāng, no one could defeat them. Yin Shen leapt into the shadows and attacked enemies from behind with her black lotus sword. A blade as dark as night, and silent like the grave. When she had her special kimono outfit no one was able to hear her coming. And when they did, it was already too late. Her sister alternatively wore very sturdy and loud armor. Drawing the attention of the enemies with her glaive. The dragons glaive, with a purifying flame that coated the blade, no evil spirit could survive its bite. (also used as a good lie detector) as a follower of tyr, Shuāng happily defended the realm from evil spirits that threatened the celestial realm. Yin shen however, only fought when she was forced to, i.e. Punishment for doing one of her pranks. Other such punishments included: practicing her languages, studying potential threats to the realm. Cleaning very fragile objects, cataloging weapons and inventory, being stuck in her fox form for the entire day. And writing apologies to those she pranked. Her sentences were usually carried out by her sister. When not reprimanding yin shen her past times included, writing poetry, practicing her techniques, trying out new kinds of tea, meditating, practising the flute, and occasionally, eating sweets. Yin shen, on the other hand, loved pranking her sister (Shuāng liked her pranks but she would never admit it outloud), sparring with her, practising magic, also playing the flute, and reading gossip from the mortal plane. And then, came her tragic mistake. A small blessing, seeming insignificant. A blessing towards other gods, including tyr and her sister, seizing the opportunity, she gave a blessing to a child to mess with the offerings. It was during a festival, the night was merry, and full of joy, she sought only to inconvenience her sister and the other gods. But the child had other plans, and yin shen was unaware. She didnt look further into the blessing, because what he asked was to be able to cast a higher tier of fire spells. She thought that possibly he was a fire dancer, or an evocation mage trying to entertain. And by the time she noticed, it was far, far too late. The night of merriment and joy turned into a night of horror and terror. Some people died, but most were injured, the festival was ruined and the offerings were destroyed. This was an egregious mistake that was no ones fault but yin shens, and she knew that well. The boy was never punished because the townspeople never found out who caused the tragedy. Yin shen remembers his face and it will forever haunt her. When the knock came at her door the next day, she knew it was not the friendly knock of her sister or a fellow god. It was a death toll for her. She stood before the court and pleaded her case, trying her best to convey that she meant no harm, it was supposed to be harmless, no one was supposed to get hurt or worse die. But the gods wouldnt listen, she could take that. But what killed her was that her sister, her family, couldnt bear to look in her direction. She didnt care about the gods, or what they thought, but she didnt want her sister to think that she was evil. She didnt want her family to think that she had this twisted dark side of her. Tyr himself came down to her, ready to deliver judgement. Without bothering or caring to hear her side, he stripped her of her title, of her special kimono armor, her magic, her immortality, and her sword. He cast yin shen out into aurils realm of frost, and scattered her items across icewind dale. And the last thing she saw was her family crying. Whether they were sad she was banished, or whether it was because they thought she was a monster, she doesnt know. And she might not ever know.

In the time following her banishment yin shen struggled, she was desperate, she almost gave up. If not for the kindness of the strangers of ten towns, she surely would have perished. She devoted her time to finding any way to get back home. And if not, to find her possessions that were banished along with her. And to reconnect herself to the weave. She survived in icewindale doing what she does best. Being sneaky, stealing, pranks, and cheating. Whether its liars dice, poker, or any other games that they played in bars. She played not for money, she knew better than to attract attention by taking their money (when they were aware of it) she won information and food, and necessities. Though she was known for her suspicious winning streak in the bar at dougans hole, people werent bothered by it considering that she never asked for money when she won. How she even got into these games without any coin to her name, that, well that shall remain a mystery. And even though she was a thief, a scoundrel, she was also a friendly face that liked to bring joy to others and help them. While she may not be famous in any sense, she was a joy to see when she entered the bar, and always brought a smile to people’s faces. She regaled tales of her time in the heavens, no one really believed her but they enjoyed hearing her stories anyway. And she loved hearing the gossip of the town. Whether it was someone dealing with an unruly customer, someone's wife cheating on their spouse or someone being wronged by their coworker, she loved hearing it. She isnt a god anymore by any means, but she still loved hearing some of the children pray to her, hoping they would succeed in causing mischief and it pained her that she couldnt give them the blessings they asked for.
