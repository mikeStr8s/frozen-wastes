# Granny Maud

_Sea hag (deceased)_

The name the thing in the caves beneath [Lac Dinneshere](../atlas/Lac%20Dinneshere.md) gave itself. The party found her on a gravelly shore by the frozen underground river, humming as she worked — a rusty cleaver rising and falling with a dull wet rhythm against a cutting board, a mound of mangled flesh sorted into grotesque piles before her, and a cauldron bubbling with something thick and foul behind.

She turned, lips peeling into a sneer.

> "Ahh, visitors. Let Granny Maud prepare you some dinner."

She was the killer of [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md)'s missing fishermen.

## Death

[Aronious](Aronious.md)'s bow came up at once, [Hobbins](Hobbins.md) hurled spells across the beach, and [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) swept in from the shadows. Under the combined assault the old woman's form split apart like shedding skin, and what emerged was no mortal crone but a towering, blue-skinned **sea hag**, twisted and wild with hunger.

The sight froze Hobbins where she stood. Aronious and Yin Shen broke the spell with sheer ferocity, and the hag collapsed into the gravel.

Yin Shen severed the head afterward as proof for the folk of Easthaven.

## What She Left

From her shrivelled hand rolled a small orb glimmering blue in the dim cave light. All three reached for it at once; only Hobbins closed her paw around it.

The [Sea Hag's Frozen Eye](../artifacts/Sea%20Hag's%20Frozen%20Eye.md).
