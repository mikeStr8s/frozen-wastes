# Oarus Masthew

_Orc, Town Speaker of Termalaine_

A large, broad-shouldered orc who carries the authority of his office the way most men carry a weapon — visibly, and without apparent effort. He dresses in fine clothing that sits oddly at odds with the rough work he seems most comfortable doing, and is rarely found behind a desk when there is something more immediate to be attended to.

When [Captain Imdra Arlaggath](Captain%20Imdra%20Arlaggath.md) sent word of the [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md) incursion and the threat gathering beneath the mountains, Masthew did not wait for further instruction. He pulled every able-bodied person in [Termalaine](../atlas/Termalaine.md) who could hold a weapon and began drilling them in the street. Most had never seen combat. He appears unconcerned by this, reasoning that inexperienced soldiers are better than no soldiers, and that experience is simply something you survive long enough to acquire.

He received the party warmly upon learning they operated under Imdra's authority, asked no unnecessary questions, and returned immediately to his militia. He strikes the party as a man who finds comfort in action and tends to trust people who demonstrate the same.

## The Kobold Arrangement

When [Foreman Lancastor](Foreman%20Lancastor.md) brought him three kobolds standing in a trenchcoat of blanket, Masthew looked at the kobolds, looked at the party, and formally accepted the arrangement on behalf of Termalaine without visible hesitation: food and shelter in exchange for work in the mines.

His militia drilling had already paid off. Termalaine repelled its own Duergar incursion and captured one of the attackers, who gave up the location of the stronghold in the [Spine of the World](../atlas/Spine%20of%20the%20World.md) under questioning. It was Masthew who told the party that Easthaven had been hit and that Imdra had been taken.
