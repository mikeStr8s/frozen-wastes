# Dale

_First mate, out of [Targos](../atlas/Targos.md)_

First mate aboard the ship the party chartered at **Three Flags Sailing** for the crossing to [Auril's Island](../atlas/Auril's%20Island.md), and the only reason that charter happened at all.

The captain — a large one-eyed man with a pipe — laughed [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) out of the room when she asked for passage onto the [Sea of Moving Ice](../atlas/Sea%20of%20Moving%20Ice.md), and told her she might as well ring [Angajuk](Angajuk.md)'s Bell. The laughing stopped when she said the voyage was meant to end the frost and that they were the ones who had killed the dragon.

The captain turned to his crew. **It was Dale who talked him into it.**

## Terms

Dale has done all the party's negotiating since, generally against the interests of his own crew.

- The crew muddled the price on purpose. Dale shook Yin Shen's hand on **thirty-five gold**, paid before sailing. The quartermaster later informed her that ten more would be due on return — more than she thought she had shaken on.
- At the island the crew refused to land and would wait one day. The party wanted two, and the quartermaster shoved the captain aside to name the price of the second: fifty gold. The haggling went on for some time, and [Aronious](Aronious.md) appealed to legend.

  **It was Dale, again, who gave.** Two days, the ten gold on return as agreed, and one magic item out of whatever the party finds on the island.
