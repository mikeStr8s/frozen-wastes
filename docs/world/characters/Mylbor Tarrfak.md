# Mylbor Tarrfak

_Dwarf, Trophy Hunter_

A barrel-chested trophy hunter with a cabin northeast of [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md), trailing a long red beard packed with snow. Encountered by the party during a blizzard while hunting a dangerous **Red Yeti** as a field test for his homemade **"Thunder Stick"** — a crude, single-shot blunderbuss of his own design, uncommon enough that the party did not recognize it on sight.

Boisterous and eager for a worthy quarry. He proposed a wager of 300 gold that he could find the Yeti before the party, which they declined. When [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) raised the prospect of hunting the Chardalyn Dragon, his interest was lukewarm until she mentioned the involvement of [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md) — at which point ancestral fury made the decision for him.

> "That certainly sweetens the deal."

## The Yeti and the Cask

He made the Bremen tavern two days later as promised, kicking the door open with a crimson-stained sack over one shoulder and the Thunder Stick in his other hand — or what was left of it, the end mushroomed outward into a tangle of singed and curling metal.

> "Only took one shot. Though I only had one shot."

He threw his purse on the bar, bought a round, and looked around the table with the expectant expression of a man waiting to be matched. [Aronious](Aronious.md) poured a full bottle of clear liquor down without pause. The barmaid put that at a cask of ale; Mylbor was behind the bar and on the floor before anyone thought to stop him, and tapped out partway through with great reluctance.

> "You win, laddy."

He and Yin Shen agreed over breakfast to stay in contact — she wanted word of the ancient white dragon, he wanted word of anything worth hunting.

## The Fortress

He answered Hobbins' _sending_ about a Duergar stronghold in the same register he answers everything, immediately and enthusiastically, and arrived at the Termalaine inn that evening by kicking the door open.

He fought through the entrance hall of Xardorok Sunblight's fortress — hemmed in and unable to bring his weight to bear in the narrow space — then cut through the throne room alongside [Polard](Polard%20Hinscrop.md) and the Shield Guardian to open the forge from the far end, raising what remained of the Thunder Stick and wading in.
