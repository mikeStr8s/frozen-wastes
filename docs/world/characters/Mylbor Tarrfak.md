# Mylbor Tarrfak

_Dwarf, Trophy Hunter_

A barrel-chested trophy hunter with a cabin northeast of [Bremen](../atlas/Bremen.md). Encountered by the party during a blizzard while hunting a dangerous Red Yeti as a field test for his homemade "Thunder Stick" — a crude, single-shot blunderbuss of his own design, uncommon enough that the party did not recognize it on sight.

Boisterous and eager for a worthy quarry. He proposed a wager of 300 gold that he could find the Yeti before the party, which they declined. When [Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) raised the prospect of hunting the Chardalyn Dragon, his interest was lukewarm until she mentioned the involvement of [Duergar](../creatures/Duergar.md) — at which point ancestral fury made the decision for him.

Agreed to meet the party at the tavern in Bremen in two days to discuss joining the hunt.
