# Henry Davis

Guard stationed at [The Caer](../atlas/The%20Caer.md). Member of the [Knights of the Black Sword](../organizations/Knights%20of%20the%20Black%20Sword.md), and by the party's reckoning the lowest-ranking one in the building.

## Party Interactions

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) cast _charm person_ on him from the foot of the castle wall, and Henry — suddenly certain these travelers were trustworthy friends — talked his captain into opening the great doors. He led the party through the cold stone corridors to the Speaker's empty office, admitted he had no idea where the Speaker actually was, and suggested they speak with the soothsayer [Hethyl Arkorran](Hethyl%20Arkorran.md).

He was still standing too close to the strangers, still smiling, when the captain arrived to find Hethyl dead on the floor.

> "Restrain them. And clap that traitor in irons."

## Unresolved

The party escaped the Caer. Henry did not go with them, and nothing in the record since says what became of him.
