# Sephek Kaltro

Criminal murderer traveling with [Torg's Trading Company](../organizations/Torg's%20Trading%20Company.md) as a guard.
## Party Interactions

The party found him in a clearing trying to catch and kill [Chwingas](../creatures/Chwinga.md) to eat. The party killed him, he dissolved into melted ice and snow leaving behind his dagger.
