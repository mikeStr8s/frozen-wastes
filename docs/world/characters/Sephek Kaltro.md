# Sephek Kaltro

A blue-eyed killer who traveled with [Torg's Trading Company](../organizations/Torg's%20Trading%20Company.md) as a caravan guard, stalked across Ten-Towns by [Hiln Trollbane](Hiln%20Trollbane.md) and said to serve some darker purpose beneath the Frostmaiden's will.

## Party Interactions

The party tracked him from [Trail's End Tavern](../atlas/Trail's%20End%20Tavern.md) through a blizzard, following footprints out of an open window and into a forest glade. They found him there in the moonlight with his blade drawn above a trembling [Chwinga](../creatures/Chwinga.md).

He fought with inhuman grace, struck down [Aronious](Aronious.md), and died with the light going out of his eyes. His body melted into the snow, leaving only his blade.

## The Dagger

The weapon shattered as he fell — and then drew itself back together, the shards fusing into a dagger whose edge carries an eerie black anti-glow, as if even the darkness feared to touch it.

[Yin Shen](Yin%20Shen.md) reached for it. It burned cold in her palm and did her no harm. It pulsed once, as if recognizing her fallen divinity, and then fell silent.
