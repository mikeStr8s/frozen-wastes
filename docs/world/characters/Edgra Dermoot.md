# Edgra Dermoot

_Human, Town Speaker of [Dougan's Hole](../atlas/Dougan's%20Hole.md)_

An old trapper woman, and the only speaker standing between the Chardalyn Dragon and two towns.

When the party worked out the dragon's route from the stone map in [Sunblight Fortress](../atlas/Sunblight%20Fortress.md) — Dougan's Hole, then [Goodmead](../atlas/Goodmead.md), then [Easthaven](../atlas/Easthaven.md), eight hours out — Goodmead had no speaker to warn. The seat had sat empty since the last one died in a verbeeg incursion. That left Edgra.

[Hobbins](Hobbins.md) cast _sending_ while [Aronious](Aronious.md) carried her at a run toward the dogsled: a dragon is coming, eight hours, get to Easthaven, and bring Goodmead's people with you.

The answer was curt. **Dougan's Hole was leaving its houses where they stood.**

She did it. Around a hundred and fifty people from the two towns reached Easthaven's gate ahead of the party, carrying what they could.

Smoke stood in the sky from the direction of Goodmead.
