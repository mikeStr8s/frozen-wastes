Session 16: The Lifespark and the Machine

The party descended through the black spire's lower opening and dropped into what had once been a wizard's laboratory — broken glass, scattered alchemical gear, and shelves long since emptied by time and ruin.

From an adjacent chamber, Yin Shen heard low voices: one robed figure speaking to an undead attendant, muttering over parchment about failed experiments and a missing lifespark. Creeping closer without entering, she heard him slam his hands down in frustration: "Dammit — all experiments failed. I need a lifespark."

After a tense whisper-circle, the party chose introductions over violence. Yin Shen knocked on the doorway before stepping in.

The robed figure turned — and the party recoiled. It was Dzaan, the same wizard Yin Shen had seen burned at the stake in Easthaven, though this one shimmered at the edges like heat-haze. "Ahh! Visitors! I am Dzaan... or almost," he said.

He explained that he was a simulacrum: incomplete, unstable, and separate from the original wizard's memories after his creation. He claimed ignorance of the execution and the crimes tied to Dzaan's name, then pointed them toward an ancient Netherese device below that could make him fully corporeal — Dzaan in body, not merely likeness.

As payment, he offered the medallion on his worktable. Yin Shen pushed further, negotiating for all information he possessed on chardalyn in addition to the amulet. Dzaan agreed.

The party agreed.

"Just a warning before going further, there are a few experiments below. Do avoid using acid, cold, or lightning while down there!"

At the next level down, Dzaan's warning proved true.

Black slimes surged from the darkness and attacked with acid-soaked pseudopods. The battle was brutal and close. Aronious's wolf was left badly burned, and Hobbins unleashed Aganazzar's scorcher in a desperate counterattack that melted through multiple oozes — but left her exposed. The final slime slammed her to the ground, unconscious and sizzling from acid burns.

Aronious and his wolf finished the last ooze while Yin Shen stabilized Hobbins. Against the odds, the party survived and pressed forward.

In a ruined bedchamber nearby, they found the corpse of a long-dead wizard and something stranger still: an invisible, locked chest hidden among the rubble.

Yin Shen failed to pick the lock, so Hobbins improvised. She had Aronious hold the unseen chest aloft while she blasted the lock with chromatic orb. The lock shattered and fell away.

Inside was a leather-bound book. The moment Yin Shen removed it, it crumbled instantly to ash.

Hobbins identified the chest as a magical container that held contents in temporal stasis — airtight, watertight, and fixed at a constant temperature. Outside the chest, the book experienced roughly two thousand years of age in moments. Deciding she was in no shape for another fight, Hobbins climbed inside for safety and pulled the lid shut.

The party, accompanied by Dzaan and his undead companion, reached the chamber of the Netherese machine. Runes were etched across the walls and apparatus, but deep cracks ran through the stone, and the inscriptions flickered with instability.

When asked to identify the runes, Hobbins could only confirm they were ancient Netherese and beyond modern understanding.

Dzaan took position on the central platform and instructed Yin Shen to touch the pedestal.

She did.

A golden bolt leapt from her hand into the machine. Runes ignited in blue and gold, the chamber roared to life, and arcane energy spiraled around Dzaan.

Then everything went wrong.

Krintas shoved the party out of the chamber as the vortex destabilized. The chest spilled open and Hobbins burst back into the chaos, demanding the door be opened. She blasted it apart with chromatic orb just as radiant energy erupted from the machine in a violent flash. Aronious reacted in time to slam the chest shut again, but Yin Shen and Aronious were struck by radiant bolts, and Krintas took the worst of it — hurled into a wall, flesh scorched and smoldering.

When the light faded, the machine wound down.

Dzaan stepped forward, now fully corporeal.

He handed over the promised amulet.

After tense questioning, the party tested Dzaan for fiendish influence. Hobbins, still under detect good and evil, saw no demonic taint on him.

They chose trust over conflict.

Dzaan agreed to travel with the party alongside Krintas. Hobbins agreed to share her spell knowledge to help rebuild his spellbook, and Dzaan offered to teach Yin Shen magic.

The group returned to the surface and reunited with Polard Hinscrop, who was disappointed to have missed the danger below.

Then Hobbins asked the question no one had dared speak aloud:

Why not use the machine again?

The entire party turned around and descended once more.

Back in the chamber, they found the damage worsening — cracks spreading, runes flickering, the structure nearing collapse. Hobbins judged there might only be one or two uses left.

They decided Yin Shen needed a ranged weapon. She created a detailed minor illusion of a blowgun with poisoned and sleep-laced darts, and Hobbins activated the pedestal.

The machine hummed, this time without the catastrophic radiant backlash.

A real blowgun and real darts clattered onto the stone floor.

"One use left, maybe," Hobbins warned.

The party fell silent, already planning what final creation might be worth the risk.
