Chardalyn Dragon

Construct (destroyed)

The crystal machine with a dragon's heart. Xardorok Sunblight's weapon, built in the forge beneath Sunblight Fortress out of stockpiled chardalyn, and intended to walk a circuit of all ten towns.

Hethyl Arkorran died foretelling it:

"Hidden away, buried in the mountains, lies a fortress, a villain, a crystal machine with a dragon's heart! O, mighty wings beating and breath like the sun! Bright enough to BURN away the Rime — to burn us all if you do... not... stop..."

In the Forge

The party saw it chained beneath an opening in the ceiling of the main forge, runic links burning against dark crystal hide, ringed by a circle of chanting Duergar whose voices were locked to the rhythm of the runes. Vast, black, and furious in the particular way of something powerful that has been made to hold still.

They broke the circle, but the chains went slack rather than holding. It raised its head, drew breath, and exhaled — not ice, but radiant light, vaporising the nearest constructs in a single sweep. Its wings snapped to full width and it launched itself up through the hole in the ceiling and north over Icewind Dale.

The Route

A relief map carved from solid stone in the upper fortress showed the plan: Ten-Towns in precise miniature, a channel cut between all ten settlements in a continuous loop, and a dragon figurine on an iron pole that a crank walked around the circuit.

It flew the route as drawn — Dougan's Hole, then Goodmead, then Easthaven. Eight hours. The party ran it in a straight line in the same eight hours and got there first.

Capabilities

  • Radiant breath that shattered a section of Easthaven's wall and took a part of the vanguard with it, leaving nothing where it had been
  • Tail, claws, and an ice-laden wing buffet that battered the Shield Guardian, Aronious, his reindeer, and the militia line together
  • Chardalyn plating that turned spears, stones, and arrows — most of what the militia threw simply bounced off

Weaknesses

It arrived wounded. Light seeped from between its plates where the Shield Guardian had hammered it in the forge — radiance leaking out of something built to hold it in — and its flight was visibly unsteady.

A militiaman got a spear under one of the plates and pried. It opened, and something bright leaked out. Hobbins's lightning chromatic orb blew a gaping wound in that flank, and a second widened it until the core was visible behind it: a small black hole, or a dead star.

"Everyone try to hit that weak spot I just exposed."

Destruction

Aronious lashed its helm-like jaw shut with thorn whip and militia hauled on the vines. The next breath had nowhere to go — it went off inside, and the chardalyn around its head cracked.

Yin Shen leapt from a rooftop onto its wing, ran up its back, and drove her shortsword into a vent in its neck, pinning the head. Aronious handed the vines off to the militia, drew the giant's bone longbow further than he ever had, and sent an arrow clean through the core.

The body began to swell. Hobbins spent her last magic on a thunderwave that drove the blast back into the dragon and away from the militia standing nearest.

It shattered into flecks too small to hold its magic, leaving a crater and a missing section of wall. Around eighty of two hundred militia were still standing.

There was nothing in the crater but chardalyn fragments.
