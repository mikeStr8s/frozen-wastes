The Ancient White Dragon

Dragon

An ancient white dragon that roams Icewind Dale and keeps at least one lair out on the Sea of Moving Ice. Still alive. Still angry.

First Sighting

The party saw it before they knew what they were looking at. In a ravine northeast of Caer-Konig they found the back half of a polar bear taken in a single bite, and then a snowdrift that shifted and fell away in a small avalanche to reveal a massive head and neck. Aronious spotted a saddle half-buried in the snow with a robed figure strapped into it, head slumped, arms hanging limp.

They crept past as quietly as they could. On the return journey it had been re-buried by fresh drifts.

The Invitation

Hobbins reached it by sending on a theory of Yin Shen's — that white dragons are benevolent creatures who enjoy the company of people.

"Don't panic, we are friendly, need help, Ten-Towns in danger, meet at Redwaters, Lupine can vouch, at sundown in two days. Ciao!"

The answer came back low and rasping, like stone grinding against stone.

"Come to my lair at the Sea of Moving Ice. You'll find a shipwreck I use as one of my homes."

Yin Shen's theory was wrong.

The Ambush

The shipwreck was a hull opened not by rot or weather but by something that had simply decided a wall was an inconvenience, the edges of the breach smooth in a way that suggested size and indifference in equal measure. Inside were a frost troll, a dragon's hoard glimmering under the ice, and four kobolds who confirmed they served it. Asked whether the dragon was friendly toward Ten-Towns, two of them had to sit down.

It arrived the way weather arrives — thunderous wingbeats, the crack of ice giving way, a shadow larger than the hull.

"Ahh. I smell the tender flesh and hear the panicked heartbeat of the meal that so eagerly delivered itself to me."

Blindness

Its eyes are filmed over, pale and clouded. It is ancient enough that its sight has largely failed, and it hunts by smell and sound.

That is the only reason the party survived. Tinjong rolled a fog cloud through the hold in a single dense surge and called them out to her, and they ran for her cave while the dragon answered its own anger with a blast of breath aimed at the sky — spears of ice coming down like thrown lances in a tight and terrible rain.

Outstanding

Yin Shen and Mylbor Tarrfak have an agreement to stay in contact: she wants word of this dragon, he wants word of anything worth hunting.

It also sits squarely between Easthaven and the northern sea route. When Dzaan suggested Revel's End as the nearest port to Auril's Island, the party pointed out there was a white dragon in the way, and went to Targos for a ship instead.
