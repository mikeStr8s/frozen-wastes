Ten-Towns

The settlements of Icewind Dale

Ten independent towns scattered around the three lakes of Icewind Dale. Each has its own Speaker; no single ruler governs them all. Yin Shen asked after the leader of all Ten-Towns early on and was told plainly that there isn't one.

That absence of central authority is the political problem running under the whole of the party's work. Warning Ten-Towns about the Duergar meant warning ten separate people, one at a time, and hoping each moved.

The Party's Standing

The party holds two written authorities, both earned rather than granted:

  • A writ from Torvus deputizing them as agents of Caer-Konig, reporting directly to the town Speaker, signed and sealed with his signet.
  • An endorsement added by Captain Imdra Arlaggath on the same document stating that while not formally deputized, they are working under official business of Captain Imdra and Easthaven.

That paper opened the door at Termalaine and got Aronious onto the wall during the dragon's assault.

Mobilisation

Imdra sent notices to every town speaker and warned it would take time, as not everyone moved as quickly as she did. She was right on both counts.

  • Termalaine mobilised immediately. Oarus Masthew drilled every able-bodied person who could hold a spear in the street, repelled its own Duergar incursion, and captured the prisoner who gave up Sunblight Fortress.
  • By the time the party returned from the black spire, three more towns had begun reinforcing militia, putting half of Ten-Towns in some state of readiness.
  • Easthaven was hit anyway — hard, fast, and with purpose — and Imdra was taken.

Other Institutions

  • Gelp — the notice boards that appeared in every town overnight and that nobody can account for
  • Torg's Trading Company — caravan haulage, travel vouchers, and a reputation for suspicious shipments
