Termalaine Mines

Gem workings, Termalaine

The tourmaline mines that put a gemstone on Termalaine's heraldry. Kobolds moved in, miners went missing, and the guild refused all work until the mine was cleared — a fifty gold notice on the Gelp board that sat unclaimed long enough for the party to walk past it twice.

A sign at the entrance read, in handwriting far too precise and deliberate to belong to any miner:

NO ENTRY — KOBOLDS ONLY

Inside

The upper tunnels reward careful attention; a few gemstones come loose from the walls with patient work. One Yin Shen pried out was valued near a hundred gold by the mine's own appraiser, who made no secret of wanting to buy it.

The workings centre on a shaft that drops away into nothing, a waterfall somewhere below filling the silence with white noise, traced by a narrow wooden walkway that is more suggestion than structure. Hobbins pressed herself to the planks, heard sawing beneath the roar, looked over the edge at two kobolds working their blades through the timber supports, and cast thunderwave straight down.

Deeper in, a chamber of gemstones clustered in cold colour — and a grell, which descended from the ceiling without sound and was dispatched by a group that had stopped being surprised by things dropping from above. Its tentacles became Yin Shen's whip, cured by Karthax.

The Possession

A crack in the stone at the far end opened onto a cramped space where the remaining kobolds had made themselves at home. One did the talking — clearly, fluently, with a precision that sat entirely wrong in a kobold's mouth, and wearing wings that were not real.

It was possessed. Something was riding behind its eyes and speaking through its throat. Hobbins' hand darted out, came away with a dirty pouch from its belt, and she walked to the edge of the central shaft and dropped it in.

The ghost erupted from the kobold like steam from a wound, shrieking, and plunged after it into the dark.

Also found here: a mind flayer's skull holding the psi crystal that would later get a nautiloid off the ground.

The Mine Now

The kobolds, freed of whatever had been steering them into banditry, wanted very little — warmth, food, and somewhere to be that wasn't a frozen mine shaft. Yin Shen offered them work in the mines for Termalaine free of charge, in exchange for meals and lodging in town.

Foreman Lancastor thought about it. Speaker Oarus Masthew accepted it.

The mine works a kobold crew now.
